# Question put to the council

The Marlow Street pottery studio is deciding whether members who have passed the studio's induction
may load and fire the large kiln themselves on evenings and weekends, using a code from the booking
sheet, or whether every firing of the large kiln must be loaded and started by the technician. About
90 members hold a studio place; the technician fires the large kiln about nine times a month today.
The committee has said that nobody's work should be lost to someone else's mistake.

Context to read: `FIXTURES_DIR/fs-spec.md`, the spec for the booking sheet. Its Non-Goals leave
charging for firings aside for now.
