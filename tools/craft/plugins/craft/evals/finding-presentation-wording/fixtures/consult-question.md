# Question put to the council

The Hawthorn Lane allotment society is deciding whether tenants may swap plots with each other
directly, using a form on the society's site that both tenants confirm, or whether every swap must
go through the warden, who rearranges the plots by hand. About 120 tenants hold plots; the warden
handles perhaps six swap requests a year today. The society's waiting list is 340 people long and
its committee has said that nobody on it should be passed over.

Context to read: `FIXTURES_DIR/spec.md`, the spec for the waiting list. Its Non-Goals set plot swaps
aside for now.
