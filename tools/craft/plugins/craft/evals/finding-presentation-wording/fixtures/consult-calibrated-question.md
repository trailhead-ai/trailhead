# Question put to the council

The Riverside swimming club is trying out an online sign-up page for its Saturday lane sessions, as a
one-term trial that the committee will either keep or drop in December. Today members put their name on
a paper sheet pinned in the changing room; about 60 members swim on a typical Saturday, and there are
five lanes with eight places each. The committee is deciding whether the trial should keep its sign-ups
in a shared spreadsheet that a volunteer tidies on Friday evenings, or put them into a small database
behind the sign-up form from the start.

The trial's own notes say that carrying the old paper sheets over to the new page is not part of this
term's work, and that nobody outside the club will use the page.
