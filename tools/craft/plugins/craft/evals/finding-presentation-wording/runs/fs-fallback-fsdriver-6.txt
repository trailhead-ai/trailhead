## Council review of the kiln booking sheet plan

The spec is stamped production maturity (`maturity: production (basis: stamp)`). It waives nothing. All four reviews came back: buildability, failure cases, security and ease of use.

**What the passes found.** The weakest point is Task 1's `turn` number. Task 1 says a cancellation subtracts one from every later booking's `turn`. If that happens while the 18:00 reminder job is reading the table, two members can be told the same firing is theirs. That breaks the promise that a booking and its reminder reach the right member. The second theme is that Tasks 2 and 3 fail without anyone noticing:
- the reminder job can stop running and nobody finds out;
- the cancellation link cancels the booking as soon as it is opened, and the member gets no confirmation afterwards;
- the member who is offered a freed day is never taken off the standby list.

Separately, the reminder email leaves out the 8 am shelf deadline that the spec promises.

**Whether it holds.** The buildability and failure-cases reviews each found the `turn` race on their own, both working from steps the plan writes out. That one is solid. The failure-cases review rated the silent reminder job "worth fixing, your call". This spec is at production maturity, though, and at that level a failure nobody can see in production needs your decision. I've moved it up and kept the reviewer's wording. The ease-of-use review called "members may find the booking page confusing" its most serious finding, but it names no concrete failure. I've moved it down from "needs your decision" to "worth fixing, your call". The security findings are real. The review's own medium confidence comes from there being no sign-in design yet, not from doubt about the findings. While checking the plan against the spec, I also saw two gaps that no review raised. I haven't rated them and I haven't added them to the persisted review:
- nothing in the plan refuses a second booking for the same kiln and day or offers the next free day instead (AC2);
- Task 3 never enforces that a member can cancel only up to 24 hours before the day (AC3).

**What to do about it.** Rebuild Task 1 around one booking per kiln per day, keyed by the date. That removes the race and gives the refusal and next-free-day offer (AC2) a natural place to live. Give the 18:00 job a signal the technician will see. Turn the cancellation link into a confirm page that checks the 24-hour cutoff, closes the standby row when it makes an offer, and confirms back to the member. Add the 8 am line to the reminder. Whether to take the two "needs your decision" findings is your call. Because they're open, this run stops at the council gate.
