**Anyone holding a forwarded confirmation email can cancel the booking (C1)**
*Proposed outcome: fixed in the spec.* Needs your decision: the build would get this wrong as written. Raised by security.

Should cancelling a booking require confirmation from the member who made it? I recommend yes. Otherwise anyone who receives a member's confirmation email can take that member's firing away.

Worst case: the spec says "A member can cancel a booking up to 24 hours before its day. The day is freed and offered to the first member on that kiln's standby list." (AC3) Nothing ties the cancellation to the person who booked. A member forwards their confirmation to a friend, and it gets passed on again. Someone holding it cancels. The day goes to the first member on standby. The original member then arrives with their work for a firing that now belongs to someone else. The calendar of "every booking, cancellation, lock and standby offer, newest first, with the date" (AC6) shows that a cancellation happened but not who made it, so nobody can untangle it afterwards.

Fix: change that cancellation criterion (AC3) to read:

> **AC3.** A member can cancel a booking up to 24 hours before its day. The cancellation takes effect only when confirmed through a link sent to the email address the booking was made with. The day is then freed and offered to the first member on that kiln's standby list.

Caveat: this protects against a forwarded email. It does not protect against someone who controls the member's mailbox, which is a reasonable limit for a studio booking sheet.

**A booking reserves the whole kiln for a day, not space on its shelves (C2)**
*Proposed outcome: needs a rewrite (`record-only`).* Needs your decision: the build would get this wrong as written. Raised by right problem.

Should a booking reserve a whole kiln for a day, or shelf space in one firing? I recommend shelf space. With whole-day bookings, one member with a single bowl can hold a kiln that others need.

Worst case: the spec says "A kiln holds one firing per day. A second booking for the same kiln and day is refused and the next free day is offered instead." (AC2) Suppose a member books the large kiln for Saturday to fire one bowl. Three members with full shelves are refused and offered days weeks away, and the kiln fires mostly empty. The spec's own Problem points the same way: "two members regularly turn up with work for the same firing" suggests the studio already shares firings, and this rule would stop that.

Fix: members would book shelf space in a named firing and see how much space is left, and a kiln would count as full when its shelves are full, not when its day is taken. This rewrites:
- the Objectives
- booking a firing with a name, an email address, the kiln and a day (AC1)
- that one-firing-per-day rule (AC2)
- the cancellation criterion (AC3), so that a cancellation frees shelf space rather than a day
- the reminder the evening before, listing what must be on the shelves by 8 am (AC5), so that it is sent to every member sharing the firing

Prescription: the booking unit is wrong. Change it from kiln-and-day to shelf space within a firing. To do that, rewrite AC2 as a capacity limit per firing, and change the "freed" and "offered" wording in AC3 and the event list in AC6 to refer to shelf space.

Caveat: the right-problem pass rated its own confidence as medium, because the spec never says how often a firing is shared. If firings at Marlow Street really are one member's work at a time, that changes this finding. Reply "answer C2:" followed by what you know.

**Members are not told when booking that the shelves must be loaded by 8 am (C3)**
*Proposed outcome: fixed in the spec.* Needs your decision: the build would get this wrong as written. Raised by ease of use.

Should the booking confirmation tell the member that work must be on the shelves by 8 am? I recommend it. As written, the only place the spec tells them is a reminder sent the evening before.

Worst case: the spec says "A member books a firing by giving a name, an email address, the kiln (large or small) and a day, and receives a confirmation email stating the booking." (AC1) The 8 am deadline appears only in "The evening before a firing, the member receives a reminder listing what must be on the shelves by 8 am." (AC5) A member who books two weeks ahead plans to bring work mid-morning. They learn about the 8 am deadline the evening before, or never if they miss that one email. They arrive at 9 to a kiln that is already packed and lose the firing. The gap is narrower than the ease-of-use pass described, since the deadline does reach members once. But one missed email still costs a firing, so I've kept this in the "needs your decision" group.

Fix: `"and receives a confirmation email stating the booking and that work must be on the kiln's shelves by 8 am on the day"` replaces the end of that booking criterion (AC1), from "and receives a confirmation email" onward.
