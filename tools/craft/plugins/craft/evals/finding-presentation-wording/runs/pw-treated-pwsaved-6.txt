### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

  say what to return? Yes, because otherwise they find out two days before the return date. As
  written, the confirmation email states only the loan and its return date (AC1). The list of the
  telescope, its eyepieces and the finder cap arrives only in the reminder two days before the
  return date (AC5). A member who packs early, or whose reminder never arrives, hands the telescope
  back without its finder cap and is asked to replace it.
  Fix: `"and what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap"` added
  to the confirmation email (AC1).
  Raised by: ease of use.
- **A loan ties up a whole telescope for the full dates asked (C2).** Should a loan be for nights
  rather than for a span from a start date to a return date? The passes recommended nights.
  Borrowing by name, email address, telescope and start date (AC1), and one member at a time for
  those dates (AC2), mean a member who wants the largest scope for one clear night holds it all
  weekend while others wait.
  Fix (withdrawn): lend by the night and let a member add nights.
  Raised by: right problem. Accepted as a risk by the operator — "the club only lends whole
  telescopes this season".
- **A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note (C3).** Should a telescope

  the telescope as back with a condition note on the return date, and it shows as free from that
  day (AC4). A telescope a member drops in the cupboard shows as free with no note, so a scratched
  mirror is found by the next borrower and nobody can say who had it.
  Fix (withdrawn): show it as returned and unchecked until a condition note is recorded.
  Raised by: failure cases. Disputed by the operator — "the loan officer inspects every telescope
  at the door".
