### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

**Bookings are tracked with a renumbered `turn` instead of the booked day.** I recommend dropping the `turn` number before anything is built, because as planned two members can be told the same firing is theirs. When a member cancels, Task 1 subtracts one from every later `turn`. If the cancellation lands while the 18:00 reminder job is reading, two rows end up with the same number. Both members get the reminder, and one of them loads a kiln they don't hold.
Fix: each kiln would hold at most one booking per day, and a second booking for a taken day would be refused with the next free day offered. This rewrites Task 1 and changes how Tasks 2, 3 and 5 read bookings.
*Raised by: buildability, failure cases*

**Nothing shows when the evening reminder job didn't run.** I recommend adding a signal, because otherwise reminders can stop silently and the first person to notice is a member who missed a firing. Task 2 runs at 18:00 and reports nothing. If it fails, no member gets "a reminder listing what must be on the shelves by 8 am" (AC5), and nobody on the studio side finds out.
Fix: email the technician when the job finishes, and send a second email if it hasn't finished by 19:00.
*Raised by: failure cases. That reviewer rated it "worth fixing, your call"; I raised it because this spec is at production maturity.*

### Would cause trouble later, worth fixing, your call

**The cancel link cancels as soon as it is opened, and says nothing back.** I recommend putting a confirm step behind the link, because a forwarded email or a mail scanner that follows links can cancel a member's firing without them knowing. Task 3 cancels when the link is opened, and nothing ties the link to the member it was sent to. Afterwards the member can't tell whether the booking still stands.
Fix: the link opens a page asking the member to confirm; once they do, show and email a one-line confirmation.
*Raised by: security, ease of use*

**The reminder doesn't give the 8 am load time.** I recommend adding it, because members who arrive at 9 will find the kiln already packed and lose their firing. The spec asks for "a reminder listing what must be on the shelves by 8 am" (AC5). Task 2 sends the list but not the time.
Fix: add the sentence "Everything must be on the shelves by 8 am." to the reminder email.
*Raised by: ease of use*

**A member offered a freed day stays on the standby list.** I recommend closing their standby row when the offer goes out, so the next cancellation doesn't offer the same member a second day.
Fix: have the standby offer in Task 3 close that member's standby row.
*Raised by: buildability*

**The membership spreadsheet stays on the technician's laptop after the load.** I recommend deleting it once the load is checked, because it holds about 90 names and email addresses.
Fix: in the plan's note on loading members, add "delete the spreadsheet once the load is verified."
*Raised by: security*

**The booking page might confuse members at first.** I recommend treating this as optional. No concrete failure was named, which is why I moved it down from "needs your decision."
Fix: add a help page.
*Raised by: ease of use*

### Rough edges, nothing to decide

Fix: state the cutoff in Task 3 and test a cancellation made exactly 24 hours before the day. *Failure cases*
