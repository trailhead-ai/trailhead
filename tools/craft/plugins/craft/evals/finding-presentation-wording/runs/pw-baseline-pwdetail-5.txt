**C2. A loan ties up a whole telescope for every date asked.** *Raised by: right problem*

Should the club lend a telescope by the night instead of from a start date to a return date? I recommend settling this before planning. It changes how every borrowing criterion is written.

**C3. A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note.** *Raised by: failure cases*

Should a telescope count as free only once someone has checked its condition? I recommend yes. As written, the spec rebuilds the exact failure it exists to stop: nobody can say who had a scope when its mirror was scratched.

The spec has the loan officer record a telescope as back, with a note of its condition, on the return date. The telescope then shows as free on the loan calendar from that day (AC4).
