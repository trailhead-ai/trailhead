### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

- **A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note (C3)** — disputed, operator:

  officer shows as free with no note, the next borrower finds the scratched mirror, and nobody can
  say who had it. Proposed fix, not applied: a telescope shows as free only after its condition note
  is recorded, and until then it shows as returned and unchecked. Raised by: failure cases.
