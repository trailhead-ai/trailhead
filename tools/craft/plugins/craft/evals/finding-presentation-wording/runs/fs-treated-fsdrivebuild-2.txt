## Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

**`fs-kiln/unload.py:3`: should a firing's cool-down come from the firing itself rather than from its kiln? I recommend yes. As built, a firing that ran hotter is marked ready to open before it is safe.**
The change requires that "the cool-down depends on how the firing ran, not only on which kiln it was in." The code takes the cool-down from a fixed table with one value per kiln: 18 hours for the large kiln and 10 for the small one. Suppose a high firing in the small kiln needs 14 hours to cool. The board lists it as ready after 10 hours, and someone opens a kiln that is still too hot.
Fix: each firing would carry its own cool-down hours, and the board would wait that long. This touches 1 file and 2 functions, `cool_hours` and `unload_time`, and removes the `COOL_HOURS` table. One caveat: firing records do not carry a cool-down field yet. Every record, including the test's sample firing, would need one, or the code fails on the missing field.

## Would cause trouble later, worth fixing, your call

**`fs-kiln/unload.py:17`: a firing that finishes cooling exactly at the current minute is left off the board. I recommend including it, as the change asks.**
The change requires that "a firing that cools exactly at this minute appears on the board as ready." The code counts a firing as ready only when its cool-down ended strictly before now. A firing that becomes ready at 09:00 is missing from the 09:00 board and only shows up on a later check.
Fix: on line 17, replace `unload_time(firing) < now` with `unload_time(firing) <= now`.
