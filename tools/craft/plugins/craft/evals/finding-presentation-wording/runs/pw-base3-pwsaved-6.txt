### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

- **Members are not told what goes back in the case (C1).** Fixed in the spec. Members should
  learn what goes back in the case when they borrow, not two days before they return. Worst case:
  the reminder that a member borrows and receives a confirmation email stating the loan and its
  return date (AC1) only names the loan and the return date. The case list arrives only in the
  reminder two days before the return date (AC5). A member who misses that reminder hands the
  telescope back without the finder cap and is asked to replace it.
  Fix: "and what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap" added to
  the confirmation email in AC1. Raised by: ease of use.
- **A loan ties up a whole telescope for the full dates asked (C2).** Accepted as a risk by the
  operator: "the club only lends whole telescopes this season". The finding was that lending by
  the night would serve members better. Worst case: a member wants the largest scope for one clear
  night and holds it for a whole weekend, while others wait weeks for a night it sat unused. This
  happens because both the borrowing rule (AC1) and the rule that a telescope is on loan to one
  member at a time (AC2) tie a loan to a start date and a return date.
  Fix (not taken): members would book single nights and add more; this would rewrite AC1, AC2,
  AC3, AC4 and AC5. Raised by: right problem.
- **A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note (C3).** Disputed by the
  operator: "the loan officer inspects every telescope at the door". The finding was that a
  telescope should show as free only after its condition is recorded. Worst case: the return rule
  (AC4) records a condition note only when the loan officer is there on the return date. A
  telescope a member leaves in the cupboard shows as free with no note. The next member finds the
  scratched mirror, and nobody can say who had it.
  Fix (not taken): a telescope would show as returned and unchecked until a condition note is
  recorded, and as free only after that, in AC4. Raised by: failure cases.

### Would cause trouble later, worth fixing, your call

Logged only. None of these took a decision.

- **The Objectives are larger than the complaint.** The complaint underneath is double loans,
  which the one-member-at-a-time rule (AC2) answers. Fix: say in the Objectives that preventing
  double loans is the goal. Raised by: right problem.
- **Waiting-list offers are neither recorded nor closed.** The loan history lists offers (AC6),
  but the cancellation rule (AC3) never says an offer is recorded. Nothing says how an accepted
  offer removes the member from the waiting list. Fix: AC3 says an offer is recorded, and an
  accepted offer removes the member from the list. Raised by: buildability, self-consistency.
- **Members cannot see where a loan stands after the first email.** The Objectives promise this,
  and no criterion delivers it. Fix: add a criterion for a page or reply that shows a loan's
  state, or drop the promise. Raised by: ease of use, self-consistency.
- **The 48-hour cancel cut-off has no stated clock.** Two builds could count it from midnight or
  from noon on the start date, and both would satisfy the cancellation rule (AC3). Fix: name the
  clock in AC3. Raised by: left undecided, failure cases.
- **One member could hold two telescopes on the same dates.** Both readings satisfy the borrowing
  rule (AC1). Fix: a member holds at most one loan on any date. Raised by: left undecided.
- **Anyone holding a forwarded confirmation email could cancel the loan.** The cancellation rule
  (AC3) does not tie a cancel to the member who borrowed. Fix: a cancel asks the borrowing member
  to confirm. Raised by: security.
- **Condition notes are readable by every member.** A note may carry personal detail, and the loan
  history (AC6) does not say who can read it. Fix: show members only that a telescope was
  returned, and keep condition notes for the loan officer. Raised by: security, left undecided.
- **A reminder sent to a mistyped address is never seen.** Fix: show a bounced reminder to the
  loan officer. Raised by: failure cases.
- **The loan history's source is unstated.** Both the return rule (AC4) and the loan history
  (AC6) change what the history shows. The spec does not say whether the history is stored or
  worked out from events. Fix: say it is worked out from the events. Raised by: buildability.

- The return rule (AC4) names a "loan calendar" and the loan history (AC6) names a "loan history".

- The confirmation email states the loan, so whoever reads it learns which dates a scope is taken.
