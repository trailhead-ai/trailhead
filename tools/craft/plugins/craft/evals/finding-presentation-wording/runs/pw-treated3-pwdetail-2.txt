**Members aren't told what goes back in the case until two days before it's due (C1)**: fixed in the spec

Should the confirmation email list what goes back in the case? I recommend it should, so the member knows from the day they collect the telescope that the finder cap has to come back.

I need to correct my summary: members are told, but only near the end. The spec already says "Two days before the return date, the member receives a reminder listing what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap" (AC5). But "A member borrows a telescope by giving a name, an email address, the telescope and a start date, and receives a confirmation email that states the loan and its return date" (AC1). So for most of the loan, nothing tells the member the cap matters. Worst case: a member loses the finder cap on the first night out because nothing said it had to come back. The reminder arrives days later, and the member is asked to pay for a new cap. If they mistyped their email address, the reminder never arrives at all.

Fix: `", and lists what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap"` added to the end of that confirmation email (AC1), so the list reaches the member when they collect the telescope as well as in the reminder.

*Raised by: ease of use. Failure cases raised the mistyped-address reminder separately, as worth fixing.*

**A loan ties up a whole telescope for the full dates asked (C2)**: needs a rewrite (`record-only`)

Should members book a telescope by the night instead of by a start date and a return date? This choice shapes the whole loan model, so it's worth settling before planning. I'm less sure of this finding than of the other two.

Both borrowing criteria tie a loan to a block of dates: the confirmation email that states the loan and its return date (AC1), and "A telescope is on loan to one member at a time. A second request for the same telescope and dates is refused and the next free start date is offered instead" (AC2). Worst case: a member who wants the club's largest scope for one clear night books it for a weekend. It sits unused on the second night while the next member waits weeks for a free date.

There's a caveat. The right-problem pass admits it doesn't know how often a loan is for a single night. The spec's own Problem says two members ask for the same telescope "on the same weekend", which suggests weekend loans are what members actually make. If you know how long loans usually run, reply "answer C2:" followed by what you know. I'll re-adjudicate with that, and the likely result is a line in the spec stating how long a loan runs.

Fix: members would book single nights and add more nights, and a scope free on Saturday would show as free even if someone has it on Friday. This rewrites the Objectives; the borrowing and one-at-a-time criteria (AC1, AC2); what a cancellation frees (AC3); and the return and reminder criteria (AC4, AC5), where the return date becomes the morning after the last night booked.

*Raised by: right problem.*

**A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note (C3)**: fixed in the spec

Should a telescope show as free before anyone has checked its condition? I recommend it shouldn't. This is the scratched-mirror case the spec was written to solve, and fixing it means changing only one criterion.

The spec says "On the return date the loan officer records the telescope as back, with a note of its condition, and the telescope shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" (AC4). The calendar shows the telescope as free from the return date, whether or not anyone recorded a note. Worst case: a member leaves the scope in the cupboard on a day the loan officer isn't in, and the calendar shows it as free anyway. The next member takes it out and finds the mirror scratched. Then the history, "The loan history lists every loan, cancellation, return and waiting-list offer for a telescope, newest first, with the date and the condition note" (AC6), has no condition note showing which loan the damage happened on. That is the exact gap the Problem describes. Because this is a production record, nobody would notice the gap until a member found the damage.

Fix: a telescope would stay off the calendar until its condition note is recorded, and in the meantime it would show as returned and unchecked. Replacement text for that return criterion (AC4):

    **AC4.** The loan officer records the telescope as back, with a note of its condition, and it
    shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day. Until that note is recorded, a telescope
    past its return date shows as returned and unchecked, and is not lent.
