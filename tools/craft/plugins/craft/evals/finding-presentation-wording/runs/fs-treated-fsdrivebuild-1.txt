**`fs-kiln/unload.py:3` — the cool-down time is set by the kiln, not by how the firing ran.**
Should each firing's cool-down come from the firing itself instead of a fixed number of hours per kiln? I recommend it, because otherwise a hotter firing gets reported as ready while it's still too hot to open.
The change says "The cool-down depends on how the firing ran, not only on which kiln it was in". The code gives every firing in the large kiln 18 hours and every firing in the small kiln 10, whatever temperature it reached. A high firing in the small kiln therefore shows "ready to unload" at 10 hours, and someone opens a kiln that isn't safe yet.
Fix: each firing would wait for its own cool-down rather than its kiln's. The firing records would need to carry their cool-down hours, and the `COOL_HOURS` table and `cool_hours` would be removed.
