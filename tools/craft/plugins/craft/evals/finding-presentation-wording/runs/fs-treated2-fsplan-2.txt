**Council review: kiln booking sheet, first build**

`maturity: production (basis: stamp)`. The spec's own stamp sets this, not a default. No concern was waived.

The four reviews come back to three themes. The first is how Task 1 models a booking. It gives each booking a `turn` number and renumbers every later row when one is cancelled. Both buildability and failure cases traced this to a concrete double-booking. Under it sits a misreading of the spec: the spec has no queue. "A kiln holds one firing per day. A second booking for the same kiln and day is refused and the next free day is offered instead (AC2)." Task 1 never refuses anything; it hands out the next number. The second theme is the cancellation and standby flow in Task 3. The link cancels as soon as it is opened. The member gets no confirmation. And a member who has been offered a day stays on the standby list. The third theme is that the evening reminder fails without anyone knowing. When it does run, it leaves out the 8 am load time the spec requires.

I agree with most of it. The double-booking is the strongest finding. Two reviewers reached it on their own, and it follows directly from the steps Task 1 writes out. I've made one change in each direction. Failure cases rated the missing signal on the reminder job as worth fixing. I've moved it up to needs your decision, because this spec is at production maturity. At that level a failure nobody can see is a decision for you, and the plan gives no reason for leaving the job unwatched. Ease of use called the booking page "confusing at first". That names no moment where a member gets stuck, so I've moved it down. The missing 8 am line is small, but it means Task 2 as written does not meet the reminder criterion. One gap no reviewer raised directly: Task 3 never enforces the rule that a member can cancel "up to 24 hours before its day" (AC3). Failure cases came closest, by noting that no test covers a cancellation made exactly 24 hours ahead.

My recommendation is one rework of Tasks 1–3 before building:
- Store the booked day with one booking per kiln per day, and drop the turn number.
- Refuse a clash and offer the next free day.
- Make the cancel link open a page that asks the member to confirm, enforce the 24-hour cutoff, then confirm the cancellation and close the standby member's row when they are offered the day.
- Add the 8 am line to the reminder and tell the technician when the 18:00 job has not finished.
The data-handling points (the membership spreadsheet, the lock reasons) are small additions to the task text.
