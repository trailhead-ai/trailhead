maturity: production (basis: stamp). No waivers.

The eight reviewers mostly found loose ends in how loans run day to day. Two places are thin. The first is the waiting list: "the dates are freed and offered to the first member on that telescope's waiting list" (AC3) never says the offer is recorded or how it gets taken up. The second is "see where the loan stands", which the Objectives promise but no criterion delivers after the first email. Under those sits one real hole in the problem the spec was written to solve. As written, "the telescope shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" (AC4) can be built so a telescope frees itself on its return date whether or not anyone has checked it. That brings back the scratched mirror nobody can trace.

The failure-cases reviewer is right about the return: the Problem names exactly this harm, and the spec is stamped production. Two other findings came in as needing your decision, and I've moved both down to "would cause trouble later, worth fixing, your call":

- **Lending by the night.** The right-problem reviewer's case rests on a guess about how often members want a scope for one night, and its own confidence line admits the spec doesn't say.
- **Listing the case contents in the confirmation email.** The ease-of-use reviewer missed that "the member receives a reminder listing what goes back in the case" (AC5) already tells the member.

The fact check refuted one fact: the club's equipment register lists seven telescopes, not six. Nothing in the spec depends on the number. "Up to 48 hours before it starts" (AC3) came back unverifiable only because the fact check treated a requirement as a claim about today. Where reviewers agreed with each other, it was on the waiting list, the undelivered "see where it stands", and the 48-hour clock. None of those blocks the build.

My recommendation is to rewrite the return criterion (AC4) so a telescope stays "returned, not yet checked" until the loan officer records its condition. Everything else gets logged rather than decided now.
