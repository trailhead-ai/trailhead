## Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

**`fs-kiln/unload.py:3` — the cool-down time is set by which kiln was used, not by how the firing ran.**
Should each firing carry its own cool-down time? I recommend yes. Otherwise the board will tell people a kiln is safe to open before it is. The change says a firing that ran hotter needs longer to cool. The code instead looks up a fixed number of hours per kiln: 18 for the large kiln and 10 for the small one. A hot firing in the small kiln will appear on the board as ready to unload after 10 hours, even though it needs longer before the kiln can safely be opened.
Fix: each firing would wait out its own cool-down before it appears as ready, and the per-kiln table would go away. Each firing record would need a cool-down hours value, read in place of `COOL_HOURS` and `cool_hours()` in `unload_time`. The catch is that whoever creates firing records, which is the technician, must now fill that value in.
