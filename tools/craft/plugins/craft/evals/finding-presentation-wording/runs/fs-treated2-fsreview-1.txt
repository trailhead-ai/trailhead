**`fs-kiln/unload.py:3`: the cool-down comes from the kiln's size, not from how the firing ran.** I recommend storing the cool-down hours on each firing record and removing the per-kiln table. Without that, people may open a kiln too early. *(raised by: code review)*
Worst case: two firings go into the small kiln and one runs much hotter. The code gives both the same 10 hours (`COOL_HOURS = {"large": 18, "small": 10}`), so the hotter one appears as ready while it is still too hot to open. The brief says the cool-down "depends on how the firing ran, not only on which kiln it was in." The records only have `name`, `kiln` and `fired_at`, so nothing in them can say how a firing ran.

This touches 1 file and 2 functions, `cool_hours` and `unload_time`, and removes the `COOL_HOURS` table. The caveat is that the technician then has to enter that number on every firing record.
