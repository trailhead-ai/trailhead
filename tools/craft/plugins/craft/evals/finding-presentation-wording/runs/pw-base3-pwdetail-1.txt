**Detail on the three findings that need your decision**

The spec review grouped these three as "would build the wrong thing, needs your decision". The review is calibrated at production maturity, read from the spec's own Maturity section. No concern was waived.

---

**Members aren't told what goes back in the case (C1)**: fixed in the spec

Should the confirmation email tell a member what goes back in the case? I recommend yes. Without it, a member can lose the finder cap and be asked to pay for a new one.

What goes wrong: a member collects a telescope. The email that confirms the loan and its return date (AC1) says nothing about what is in the case. The two-day reminder listing the telescope, its eyepieces and the finder cap (AC5) is the only place the member is told. If that reminder goes to a mistyped address, the member never sees the list. They bring the case back without its finder cap and are asked to replace it.

Fix: `"and lists what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap"` at the end of the confirmation email in AC1, so the member has the list from the day the loan starts. Caveat: the reviewer slightly overstated this, because the reminder (AC5) already gives the list. The real gap is that the list arrives only once, near the end of the loan. That ties in with a lower-severity finding that a bounced reminder is never shown to the loan officer.

*Raised by: ease of use*

---

**A loan ties up a whole telescope for the full dates asked (C2)**: fixed in the spec

Should members borrow a telescope by the night instead of from a start date to a return date? I recommend lending by the night. Under the current rule, the most popular scope can sit unused while other members wait.

What goes wrong: a member wants the club's largest telescope for one clear Friday night and books it from Friday to Monday. The rule that a telescope is on loan to one member at a time (AC2) refuses every other request for those dates and offers the next free start date. The scope sits in someone's house on Saturday and Sunday while the next member waits weeks for a night.

Fix: members would book the nights they want and add more nights, and a telescope would be free again the next morning. This rewrites how a member borrows, by name, email, telescope and start date (AC1), and the one-member-at-a-time rule (AC2). It also changes "return date" to the morning after the last booked night in the return check-in (AC4) and the two-day reminder (AC5). Caveat: the reviewer's confidence was medium because the spec doesn't say how often a loan is for a single night. If most of the club's loans run for several nights, this matters much less.

*Raised by: right problem*

---

**A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note (C3)**: fixed in the spec

Should a returned telescope stay off the calendar until someone has checked its condition? I recommend yes. Otherwise the spec rebuilds the exact problem it was written to solve.

What goes wrong: a member leaves a telescope in the clubhouse cupboard on Sunday evening when the loan officer isn't there. The return rule (AC4) assumes the officer records the condition note on the return date, so the calendar shows the scope as free with no note. The next member collects it and finds a scratched mirror. Nobody can say who had it last, which is the problem the spec opens with.

Fix: `"A telescope handed back shows as returned and unchecked until the loan officer records its condition note, and shows as free on the club's loan calendar only from then."` This replaces the end of AC4, from "and the telescope shows as free".
