**Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision**

1. **Should the queue stop storing a place number? I recommend yes, because as planned one freed plot can be offered to two people.**
   Worst case: someone leaves the Hawthorn Lane queue just as the 02:00 offer job runs. Task 1 is partway through shifting every later place down by one, so the job sees two rows at place 1, and both people get an offer email for the same plot. That breaks the rule that the person at the front of the queue is offered the plot (AC2). It also breaks the rule that everyone behind someone who leaves moves up one place (AC5).
   Fix: store an ordering timestamp per row and work out place from it when reading. A lapsed offer sends the person to the back (AC3) by resetting their timestamp to now. The confirmation email (AC1) shows the place worked out at join time.

   Worst case: the warden runs the import twice, and every person ends up in the queue twice. Or the run stops halfway on the laptop, and nothing records which rows went in. Either way the live queue is wrong, with no clean way to repair it.
   Fix: import under a batch id. Show a preview of what will be inserted before writing anything, and add a remove-this-batch step so a bad run can be cleared out completely.
   *Raised by: failure cases*

**Would cause trouble later, worth fixing, your call**

3. **An out-of-turn grant leaves the person in the queue, so they'll be offered a second plot.
