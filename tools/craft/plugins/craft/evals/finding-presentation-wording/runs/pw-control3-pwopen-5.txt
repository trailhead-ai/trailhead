**What the passes found.** The most serious problem is in the returns criterion: a telescope can show as free before anyone has checked it. Several rules are left open: how long a loan runs, since borrowing asks only for a start date (AC1); how many scopes one member may hold at once; which clock the 48 hours run on; who may cancel; and who may read condition notes. The fact check also found that the club has seven telescopes, not six.

The fact check marked the 48-hour cancellation claim unverifiable, but that sentence is a requirement, not a claim about today, so there's no finding there.
