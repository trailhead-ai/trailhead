**Gauntlet: telescope loan desk spec (draft), round 1**
maturity: production (basis: stamp). No stand-downs.

**What the passes found.** The reviewers kept coming back to two things. The first is the return. The Problem says nobody can tell who had a scope when its mirror was scratched, yet the spec never says what happens when nobody checks a telescope in. The second is the club's emails. Most of what the spec promises reaches members by email: the confirmation, the reminder with the case list, and the waiting-list offer. Nothing says what happens when one of those emails never arrives. Around these two are smaller gaps:
- The Objectives promise that a member can "see where the loan stands", but no criterion delivers it.
- Waiting-list offers are never closed or recorded.
- The cancellation window is unpinned.
- The spec doesn't say who may cancel a loan, or who may read the condition notes.

**Whether it holds.** The failure-cases reviewer was right about the unchecked return, and the spec's own Problem backs it. That reviewer rated the undelivered-email gap as only worth fixing. I've raised it to needs your decision: this spec is stamped production, and at production a failure no human would see is a decision for you. It also breaks the Objective that a loan "reach the right member". Two other "needs your decision" findings didn't hold up, and I've moved both to worth fixing, your call:
- **Ease of use** said members are never told what goes back in the case. The two-days-before reminder already lists it (AC5). What's left is the reminder going unseen, and that is covered by the email decision.
- **Right problem** proposed lending by the night. It rests on a guess about how often members want a single night, which its own confidence line admits. The Problem's actual complaint is about weekends.

The right-problem reviewer's claim that six criteria are more than the complaint needs also overreaches. The Problem names three complaints, not one.

The fact check found that the equipment register lists seven telescopes, not six. Nothing in the spec relies on the number.

**What to do.** Make two edits and advance:
- Rewrite AC4 so a telescope shows as free only once someone records its condition.
- Add one criterion that shows the loan officer any email that couldn't be delivered.

The worth-fixing gaps can be pinned now or left for planning. None of them changes what the spec commits to.
