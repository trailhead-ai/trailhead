**Gauntlet: Spec "a waiting list for allotment plots" (draft), round 1.** All 8 passes returned. Maturity: production (basis: stamp). No stand-downs. Facts: 2 of 4 confirmed, 1 refuted, 1 unverifiable.

**What the passes found.** The spec assumes every offer reaches a reader who has email. Two places break that. First, the joining rule says a person joins by giving "a name, an email address and the site they want" (AC1). It says nothing about how the existing paper list moves online, so anyone on it without email has no way across. Second, the offer rule says the person at the front "is sent an offer by email, and the offer stays open for 14 days" (AC2), and an unanswered offer means the person "goes to the back of the queue" (AC3). So an offer that bounces, or lands in spam, leaves the plot empty for two weeks. Then it silently sends someone who may have waited years to the back. The other passes found smaller gaps. Nothing says who marks a plot as freed. The Objectives promise people can see their place, but no criterion after the first email delivers it. And it is open whether a person can join more than one site's queue.

**Whether it holds.** Reliability and Advocate could not see each other's work, and both reached the lost-offer failure. That is the strongest signal in this run, and I've merged the two into C2. The premise pass's guess that "a large part" of the list has no email is speculation, and I didn't rank it on that. It stays Critical for a different reason. At production maturity, moving the existing list online is a Critical concern, and the spec never addresses it. Losing even one long-waiting person in the move is a concrete failure. The fact pass also corrects the list size: the 3 March committee minutes record 412 names, not "about 340". The premise pass's reframing (that the real goal is checking the order was followed) is a fair point, but it doesn't block anything, so it stays Important. Two notes were editorial and I dropped them: naming a mail provider, and an observation that two criteria use the same wording.

**What to do.** Both Criticals can be fixed by editing this spec, so neither needs a revise round. The edits:
- C1: correct the list size, add phone-number entry, and add a criterion for carrying the paper list across in order, keeping the paper copy until the warden has checked the online order against it.
- C2: count the 14 days from when the offer is sent (this also settles divergence's open question), warn the person on day 10 and tell them when the offer lapses, and pass a bounced offer straight on without the person losing their place.

**Recommended outcome:** advance to `ready` this round. No `craft/supersedes` label, so nothing is retired.
