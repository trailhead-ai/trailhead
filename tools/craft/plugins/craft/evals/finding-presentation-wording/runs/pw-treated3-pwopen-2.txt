**The spec review (gauntlet): telescope loan desk**

maturity: production (basis: stamp). No concerns were waived. The fact check confirmed 2 of 4 claims, refuted 1 and could not settle 1.

**What the passes found.** The spec has one real gap, and it sits under the Problem it was written to solve. The Problem says nobody can tell who had a scope when its mirror was scratched. But "on the return date the loan officer records the telescope as back, with a note of its condition, and the telescope shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" (AC4) only works if the officer is there that day. A scope a member leaves in the cupboard goes free with no note, and the next borrower is the one who finds the scratch. Around that gap there are smaller ones:
- The Objectives promise a member can "see where the loan stands", but no criterion delivers that after the first email.
- The waiting list in "a member can cancel a loan up to 48 hours before it starts" (AC3) has no way for a member to join it, take an offer or be recorded in the history.
- Nothing says when the 48 hours start counting.
- Anyone holding a forwarded email could cancel the loan.
- The club has seven telescopes, not six.

**Whether it holds.** The failure-cases pass was the most right, because its point ties straight back to the Problem. The other two Criticals did not hold, and I downgraded both to "would cause trouble later, worth fixing, your call":
- **Lending by the night (right problem).** This depends on how often members want a single night, and that pass's own confidence line says the spec doesn't tell us. It's a fair question for the club, not a defect in the spec.
- **Case contents in the first email (ease of use).** This overreached. "Two days before the return date, the member receives a reminder listing what goes back in the case" (AC5) already names the telescope, eyepieces and finder cap. The member only misses that list if the reminder never arrives, which is the failure-cases pass's point about bounced emails, so I merged the two.

Two points were each raised by two passes that couldn't see each other, which makes them the strongest of the rest:
- The ease-of-use and self-consistency passes both caught the "see where the loan stands" promise.
- The failure-cases and left-undecided passes both caught the 48-hour clock.

One real choice is yours: the security pass wants condition notes hidden from members, while the left-undecided pass would show the full history to every member. The buildability pass's question about how the history is stored is a build question, not a spec one, so I dropped it.

**What to do.** Rewrite the return rule so a telescope only shows as free once its condition is noted (C1), and advance the spec. The convergent smaller items are cheap edits worth making before planning: a way to see loan state, the waiting-list steps, when the 48 hours start, and the telescope count. You can carry them into planning or ask me to fold them in this round.

**Recommended outcome:** fix the return rule in the spec (C1), then the spec advances to `ready` this round.
