**Council review: kiln booking sheet, first build**

maturity: production (basis: stamp). No concern was waived.

**What the reviewers found.** Three things stand out. First, the ordering in Task 1 is unsafe. Every booking carries a `turn` number, and cancelling a booking renumbers every later one. If that renumbering overlaps the 18:00 reminder job, two members can each be told the same firing is theirs. That breaks AC2's promise of one firing per kiln per day, and it breaks the objective that a booking reaches the right member. Second, the reminder job in Task 2 can stop without anyone knowing. Its email also leaves out the 8 am shelf deadline that AC5 requires. Third, the cancellation in Task 3 is too easy to trigger and gives no answer back. One click on a link cancels the booking. A mail scanner or a forwarded email can do that click. The member gets no confirmation, and whoever was offered the freed day stays on the standby list.

**How well the findings hold up.** The buildability and failure-cases reviewers each found the `turn` race on their own. Task 1 spells out the renumbering, so this one is solid. The failure-cases reviewer rated the silent reminder job only "worth fixing". I've moved it up to "needs your decision": the spec is stamped production, and at that level a failure nobody can see is rated that high. Members would find out only when they miss a firing. The ease-of-use reviewer's main concern, that the booking page "may be confusing", names no moment where a member gets stuck, so I moved it down. Their point about the 8 am deadline is the sharper one, because the plan as written doesn't fully meet AC5. The security reviewer's points are real and specific. They noted lower confidence only because no sign-in design exists yet. One more gap: Task 3 doesn't state AC3's 24-hour cutoff. That fits with the failure-cases reviewer's request for a test at exactly 24 hours.

**What I recommend.** Make two changes before building. Hold each booking by its kiln and day, with no `turn` number. Have the reminder job report to the technician when it runs and when it doesn't. Then rework Task 3 in one pass: a confirm page behind the link, the 24-hour cutoff, a confirmation to the member, and closing the standby row once a day is offered. Add the 8 am sentence to the reminder email. Add a line to the Notes saying the membership spreadsheet is deleted after the import. Everything else is small.

As planned, two members can be told the same firing is theirs.** A member cancels while the 18:00 reminder job is reading the table. *Raised by: failure cases*
