**The spec review (gauntlet) of the telescope loan desk spec: ready to advance once two edits are applied**

maturity: production (basis: stamp). No waivers.

The passes kept returning to one thing the spec cares about: knowing what happened to each telescope. The failure-cases pass found a hole in the returns rule. The spec says that "on the return date the loan officer records the telescope as back, with a note of its condition, and the telescope shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" (AC4). A scope left in the cupboard when the officer isn't there therefore shows as free with no note. That is the scratched-mirror problem the spec was written to solve. Separately, the self-consistency and ease-of-use passes both found that the Objectives promise a member can "see where the loan stands", but no criterion delivers it after the first email. The rest of the findings cluster around waiting-list offers, the 48-hour cancel, and who can read the history.

I think the returns finding and the loan-status finding both hold. The loan-status gap came from two passes that couldn't see each other, which is the strongest signal this review has. Two other findings overreached:
- **Lending by the night.** The right-problem pass proposed lending each scope a night at a time. That depends on how often members want a single night, and its own report says the spec doesn't record that. It's a guess about behaviour, so I've moved it down from "needs your decision" to "worth fixing, your call".
- **Case contents in the confirmation email.** The ease-of-use pass wanted the confirmation email to list what goes back in the case. It missed that the reminder sent two days before the return date already lists "the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap" (AC5). It's moved down too.

The fact check refuted one line. The club's equipment register lists seven telescopes, not six. Nothing in the spec depends on the number, but the Problem section should be corrected.

My recommendation: apply the two edits below and advance. A telescope only shows as free once someone has written a condition note. A member can look up their own loan's state at any time. The remaining findings are yours to take or leave; none would change what gets built.

**Recommended outcome:** this round, the spec becomes `ready`.
