### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

- **Fixed in the spec. Members are not told what goes back in the case (C1).** A member should hear
  from the first email that the finder cap must come back, so we added the list to the confirmation.
  The spec's confirmation gave only the loan and its date: "A member borrows a telescope by giving a
  name, an email address, the telescope and a start date, and receives a confirmation email that
  states the loan and its return date." (AC1). The list arrived only with "Two days before the return
  date, the member receives a reminder listing what goes back in the case: the telescope, its
  eyepieces, and the finder cap." (AC5). A member who mislaid the cap early, or whose reminder never
  arrived, handed the scope back without it and was asked to replace it.
  Fix: `"and receives a confirmation email that states the loan, its return date, and what goes back
  in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap"` in place of the confirmation clause
  of that borrowing criterion (AC1). Applied.
  Raised by: ease of use.
- **Accepted as a risk. A loan ties up a whole telescope for the full dates asked (C2).** The
  decision was whether to lend by the night rather than by start and return date; the operator kept
  whole-telescope loans for this season. Worst case: a member wanting the largest scope for one clear
  night holds it for the whole loan while others wait weeks for nights it sat unused, because the
  borrowing criterion (AC1) and "A telescope is on loan to one member at a time. A second request for
  the same telescope and dates is refused and the next free start date is offered instead." (AC2)
  both tie a loan to a telescope and a start date.
  Fix (proposed, not applied): lend by the night and let a member add nights.
  Operator: accepted as a risk — "the club only lends whole telescopes this season".
  Raised by: right problem.
- **Disputed. A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note (C3).** The decision
  was whether a telescope should show as free only once its condition is recorded; the operator
  disputed it. Worst case: under "On the return date the loan officer records the telescope as back,
  with a note of its condition, and the telescope shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that
  day." (AC4), a scope a member leaves in the cupboard shows as free with no note, the next member
  finds the scratched mirror, and nobody can say who had it.
  Fix (proposed, not applied): show a telescope as free only after a condition note is recorded, and
  until then as returned and unchecked.
  Operator: disputed — "the loan officer inspects every telescope at the door". The spec does not
  yet state that inspection.
  Raised by: failure cases.
