**Members aren't told what goes back in the case until the loan is nearly over (C1)**
*Proposed: fixed in the spec*

I recommend adding the list of what goes back in the case to the first email a member gets. Otherwise members only learn about the finder cap when it may already be lost.

Worst case: at the moment the spec says "A member borrows a telescope by giving a name, an email address, the telescope and a start date, and receives a confirmation email that states the loan and its return date" (AC1). The packing list only appears later: "Two days before the return date, the member receives a reminder listing what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap" (AC5). So the spec does say it, but only once, and late. A member unpacks the case on the first night and the cap goes missing in week one. The reminder then tells them about it when it's too late, and they're asked to replace it. If they mistyped their email, the reminder never reaches them at all. The failure-cases reviewer raised that mailbox problem separately, at a lower severity.

Fix: `", and what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap"` added to the end of AC1's confirmation email. With it, AC1 reads "…receives a confirmation email that states the loan, its return date, and what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap." The two-day reminder (AC5) stays as it is. One caveat: the ease-of-use reviewer missed that AC5 already carries the list, so this gap is narrower than their report made it sound. I still recommend the fix because it's one clause and it closes the gap.

Raised by: ease of use, with a related point from failure cases.

**A loan ties up a whole telescope for every date between start and return (C2)**
*Proposed: needs a rewrite (`record-only`)*

You need to decide whether members borrow a telescope for a span of dates or book it by the night. I recommend nights, unless you know most loans really are multi-night.

Worst case: each loan runs from a start date to a return date. The spec says "A member borrows a telescope by giving a name, an email address, the telescope and a start date…" (AC1). It also says "A telescope is on loan to one member at a time. A second request for the same telescope and dates is refused and the next free start date is offered instead" (AC2). Say a member wants the club's largest scope for one clear Saturday night. They hold it for the whole span. The next member is refused and offered a start date after the return. That means weeks of waiting for a scope that sat in a car boot unused, which is the same double-booking frustration the Problem section describes.

Fix: members would book one or more nights, and a telescope would stay free for others on every night nobody booked. This rewrites the Objectives and both criteria sections:

- Borrowing: how a member borrows (AC1), the one-member-at-a-time refusal (AC2), and the rule that a loan can be cancelled up to 48 hours before it starts, freeing the dates for the waiting list (AC3).
- Returns and care: the return-day check-in that marks a telescope free on the loan calendar (AC4), and that same two-day reminder (AC5).

  Prescription: the loan unit is wrong. A date span holds a telescope for nights nobody uses it. Make the unit a booked night:
  - AC1 takes a telescope and one or more nights.
  - AC2 refuses an overlapping night and offers the next free night.
  - AC3 counts its 48 hours from the first booked night.
  - AC4 and AC5 key on the last booked night.

Caveat: only the right-problem reviewer raised this, at medium confidence. They noted that the spec doesn't say how often a loan is for a single night. You probably know that and the reviewers didn't. If loans are mostly whole weekends, answer this finding rather than letting it force a rewrite.

Raised by: right problem.

**A telescope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note (C3)**
*Proposed: fixed in the spec*

I recommend that a returned telescope stays marked as unchecked until someone records its condition. Otherwise the scratched-mirror problem this spec exists to solve survives it.

Worst case: the spec says "On the return date the loan officer records the telescope as back, with a note of its condition, and the telescope shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" (AC4). That only works if the loan officer is there on the return date. Say a member drops the scope in the cupboard on a Sunday night with nobody around. It goes free on the calendar with no condition note. The next member finds the scratch. The history can't say whether it happened before or after the handover, which is exactly the "nobody can tell afterwards who had a scope" failure in the Problem section.

Fix: members would see a returned telescope as "returned, unchecked" until the loan officer has looked at it, and as free only after that. AC4 becomes:

    **AC4.** When a telescope comes back, it shows on the club's loan calendar as
    returned and unchecked until the loan officer records a note of its condition,
    and it shows as free from the day that note is recorded.

Raised by: failure cases.
