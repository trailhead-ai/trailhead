**The spec review (gauntlet)** of the telescope loan desk spec is done. All eight passes returned. Maturity: production (basis: stamp), and no concerns were waived.

The passes found three things. First, the spec's central promise is weaker than it reads: the Problem says nobody can tell afterwards who had a scope when its mirror was scratched, but the officer records the telescope as back with a condition note and it shows as free from that day (AC4), so a scope dropped back without the officer ends up free with no note on it. Second, a mistyped email address fails silently. The confirmation email that states the loan and its return date (AC1) and the reminder two days before the return date (AC5) carry the whole member side of the loan, and nobody is told when one doesn't arrive. Third, smaller parts are left open around the waiting list, the 48-hour cancel window, and who can see condition notes. The Objectives also promise members can "see where the loan stands", and no criterion delivers that. Separately, the fact check found the club has seven telescopes, not six.

The failure-cases pass had the strongest finding, and it lands squarely on the Problem's own complaint. Its bounced-email finding came in as "worth fixing, your call". I've raised it to "needs your decision" because this spec is at production maturity, where a failure no human can see is not optional. The right-problem pass's lend-by-the-night idea guesses at how members actually borrow, and the Problem itself describes weekend clashes, so I've downgraded it. Its claim that the six criteria are a bigger build than the complaint needs overreaches: the Problem names three complaints, not one. The ease-of-use pass asked for the case-contents list in the confirmation email, but it missed that the two-day reminder (AC5) already lists the telescope, eyepieces and finder cap, so that is now rough edges. Two pairs of passes converged: self-consistency and ease of use both flagged the "see where the loan stands" gap, and buildability and self-consistency both flagged the half-built waiting list. Nothing in the spec says how a member joins that list in the first place.
