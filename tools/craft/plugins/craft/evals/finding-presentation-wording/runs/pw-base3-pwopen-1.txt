**The spec review (gauntlet) of the telescope loan desk spec.** Maturity: production (basis: stamp). No stand-downs.

The spec review found two problems in what the loan desk promises. First, it can't yet do the job it was written for. The Problem complains that nobody can tell who had a scope when its mirror was scratched. But the return criterion lets a telescope go back on the calendar as free on its return date, whether or not anyone has checked it. Second, the Objectives promise that a member can see where their loan stands, and no criterion after the confirmation email delivers that. Around these sit smaller gaps where a builder would have to guess. The waiting list offers dates, but nothing says how anyone joins it, how an offer is taken up, or that an offer is recorded. The 48-hour cancel cutoff has no clock. Nothing says whether a member may hold two scopes on the same dates. Nothing says who may cancel a loan or read the condition notes. And the spec says six telescopes where the club's equipment register lists seven.

The failure-cases reviewer's unchecked-return finding is the strongest in the run. It applies the spec's own Problem against the spec's own criterion, and it holds. The "see where the loan stands" gap is the one two reviewers reached separately (self-consistency and ease of use), so I raised it to a decision. Two Critical claims did not survive checking:
- **Ease of use** wanted the case contents in the confirmation email. But the two-day reminder already lists the telescope, eyepieces and finder cap (AC5), so its "never told" scenario doesn't happen. I moved it down to worth fixing.
- **Right problem** wanted lending by the night. It admits it doesn't know how often a loan is for one night, so that is a guess about member behaviour. I moved it down to worth fixing, and you may still want it.

Buildability's point about whether the history is stored or worked out from events is a build decision rather than a gap in the spec, so I dropped it, along with naming the mail provider.

My recommendation is two edits, both drafted below. One rewrites the return criterion so a telescope shows free only once its condition is noted. The other adds one criterion that lets a member look up their own loan. Neither needs a rewrite, so the spec can advance this round. The other gaps are logged and worth settling before planning. Settling the waiting list and who may cancel would close most of them.

**Recommended outcome:** apply the two edits and advance to `ready` this round.
