### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

**One freed plot can be offered to two people. I recommend replacing the stored place number with join-time ordering.**
Suppose someone leaves while the 02:00 job is running. Every later `place` drops by one partway through the read, two rows end up holding place 1, and both people get an offer for the same plot.
Fix: store the join time, order by it, and never store a place number. The place in the confirmation email is calculated when the email is sent.
*Raised by: buildability, failure cases*

**The paper-list import can't be previewed or undone. I recommend importing under a batch id with a preview.**
If the import runs twice, everyone's row is doubled. If it stops halfway, nothing shows where it stopped. With 340 people's places at stake on a production system, there is no clean way back.
Fix: run a preview first, tag every imported row with a batch id, and make it possible to remove a bad batch in one step.
*Raised by: failure cases. Rated at the top level by this spec's production maturity for migration and backfill.*

**If the nightly offer job stops running, nobody finds out. I recommend having it report to the warden.**
The job fails one night, and offers quietly stop. The first sign is people writing in to ask why they've heard nothing.
Fix: email the warden when the job finishes, and send an alert if it hasn't finished by 03:00.
*Raised by: failure cases. The reviewer rated it one level lower; I raised it because the production calibration puts "you'd never know it failed" at the top level.*
