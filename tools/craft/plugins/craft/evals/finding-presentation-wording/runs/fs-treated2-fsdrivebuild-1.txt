**Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision (1)**

`fs-kiln/unload.py:3 — the cool-down hours come from a table keyed by kiln size, so a firing that ran hotter in the same kiln is marked ready too early`
How long a firing must cool should depend on the firing, not just the kiln. I recommend storing each firing's cool-down hours on the firing itself, because otherwise the board can tell someone to open a kiln that is still too hot. The change says the cool-down depends on how the firing ran, not only on which kiln it was in. The code gives every firing in a kiln the same wait: 18 hours for the large kiln, 10 for the small one. A firing that ran hotter in the large kiln appears as "ready to unload" after 18 hours, even though it needs longer.
Fix: each firing would wait out its own cool-down, so a hotter firing stays off the board until it is safe to open. This touches 2 files and 3 functions, mainly `cool_hours` and `unload_time`. It also removes the `COOL_HOURS` table and adds the new field to the test's firing record.

**Would cause trouble later, worth fixing, your call (1)**

`fs-kiln/unload.py:17 — a firing that cools exactly at this minute is left off the list`
A firing whose cool-down ends this minute should count as ready, and I recommend counting it. The change says such a firing appears on the board as ready, but the check uses a strict "earlier than now." If the board is built at the exact minute a firing finishes cooling, that firing is missing until the next run.
Fix: `unload_time(firing) <= now` in place of `unload_time(firing) < now` in `ready_firings`, so a firing that finishes cooling this minute is listed as ready.
