### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

- **Members are not told what goes back in the case (C1).** Fixed in the spec. Should the first
  email a member gets list what has to go back in the case? We recommend yes, because otherwise
  members lose parts they were never told about. Worst case: a member reads only the confirmation

  told them to keep. The list currently arrives only in the reminder two days before the return
  date (AC5).
  Fix: "and what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap" added to
  the confirmation email in "A member borrows a telescope by giving a name, an email address, the
  telescope and a start date, and receives a confirmation email that states the loan and its return
  date" (AC1).
  Raised by: ease of use.
- **A loan ties up a whole telescope for the full dates asked (C2).** Accepted as a risk by the
  operator: "the club only lends whole telescopes this season". Should a loan be booked by the night
  rather than from a start date to a return date? We proposed yes, because one clear night can
  otherwise cost everyone else a weekend. Worst case: a member wants the club's largest scope for
  one clear night, holds it for the whole weekend, and others wait weeks for a night the scope spent
  unused. Both "A member borrows a telescope by giving … a start date" (AC1) and "A telescope is on
  loan to one member at a time. A second request for the same telescope and dates is refused and
  the next free start date is offered instead" (AC2) tie a loan to start and return dates.
  Fix (withdrawn by the override): members could book the single nights they want and add more;
  this would rewrite the borrowing criteria (AC1, AC2).
  Raised by: right problem.

  operator: "the loan officer inspects every telescope at the door". Should a telescope show as free
  only after its condition has been noted? We proposed yes, because the scratched mirror in the
  Problem section can't otherwise be traced. Worst case: a member leaves a telescope in the cupboard

  Fix (withdrawn by the override): a telescope back without a note would show as "returned and
