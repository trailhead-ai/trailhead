The spec review (gauntlet) of the Hollin Hill telescope loan desk spec: all eight reviewers have reported. Maturity: production (basis: stamp). No concerns were waived.

The spec mostly holds up. The reviewers found two problems serious enough to need your decision, and both are about what happens when a loan doesn't end cleanly. The Problem says nobody can tell who had a scope when its mirror was scratched. But the criterion where "the loan officer records the telescope as back, with a note of its condition" (AC4) only works if the officer is there on the return date. A scope a member leaves in the cupboard shows as free with no note, which is the same failure the spec was written to fix. The second problem is emails that never arrive. The confirmation and the reminder "listing what goes back in the case" (AC5) both depend on the address being typed correctly, and nothing tells anyone when they bounce. Below those two, several reviewers kept landing on the same gaps: the promise that a member can "see where the loan stands" has nothing delivering it, and the waiting-list offer in the cancellation criterion (AC3) is never closed or recorded.

The failure-cases reviewer was right on both serious points. It rated the bounced-email point as only worth fixing. I raised it to needs your decision because the spec is at production maturity, where a failure nobody can see is rated that way. The right-problem reviewer's idea of lending by the night is worth considering. But its own confidence note says nobody knows how often a loan is for one night, so it rests on a guess about members and I moved it down to "worth fixing, your call". The ease-of-use reviewer wanted the finder-cap list added to the confirmation email, but it missed that the reminder (AC5) already carries that list. The real risk is the reminder not arriving, which is the bounced-email problem, so that one moved down too. The fact check found one wrong fact: the club's equipment register lists seven telescopes, not six. That's a one-word fix, and the spec doesn't depend on the number anywhere else.

My recommendation is to make two edits and let the spec advance. Change AC4 so a scope that comes back without a note shows as unchecked, not free. Add a seventh criterion so an undelivered email shows up for the loan officer. The "worth fixing" items below are yours to take or leave. I'd at least correct the telescope count and settle the "see where the loan stands" wording.
