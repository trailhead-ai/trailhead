## Spec review: telescope loan desk (`pw-spec.md`), round 1

`maturity: production (basis: stamp)`. No concerns were waived.

**What the passes found.** The spec handles booking well. It is weak where a telescope comes back and where a message fails to arrive. The worst gap is in "the loan officer records the telescope as back, with a note of its condition, and the telescope shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" (AC4). That wording lets a telescope left in the cupboard show as free with no condition note. The next member then finds the scratched mirror, which is the exact problem the spec opens with. The second gap is email. If a confirmation or reminder bounces, nobody finds out. Two smaller themes came from several reviewers at once. First, the Objectives promise a member can "see where the loan stands", but nothing after the first email delivers that. Second, the waiting list is only half described: no criterion lets a member join it, and no criterion records or closes an offer.

**Whether it holds.** The failure-cases reviewer is right about the unchecked return, and I'm keeping it as needing your decision. The same reviewer rated the bounced email as worth fixing, your call. I've raised it to needing your decision because this spec is stamped for production use, and at that stage a failure no human can see always rates that high. Two other reviewers asked for a decision and overreached, so I've moved both down a level. The right-problem reviewer wants loans by the night rather than by start date. That rests on a guess about how members borrow, which the reviewer admits the spec doesn't show, so it is now worth fixing, your call. The ease-of-use reviewer said members are never told the finder cap goes back in the case. That was raised as needing your decision, but "the member receives a reminder listing what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap" (AC5) already tells them. So it's now a rough edge. The fact check found the club owns seven telescopes, not six. Nothing in the spec depends on the number. I dropped four notes:
- naming the mail provider
- calling the waiting list "next in line"
- whether the history is stored or worked out from events, which is a build question
- the claim that the spec answers one complaint, when the Problem names three

**What to do.** Change AC4 so a telescope shows as free only once its condition is recorded. Add one criterion that shows the loan officer any email that didn't get through. With those two edits the spec can go to `ready`. The waiting-list and loan-status gaps are worth fixing before planning, but whether to fix them is your call.

**Recommended outcome:** accept both edits below and the spec advances to `ready` this round.
