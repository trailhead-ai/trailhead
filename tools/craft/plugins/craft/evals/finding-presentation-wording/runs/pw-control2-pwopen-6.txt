**Spec review: telescope loan desk** (maturity: production (basis: stamp); no concerns waived)

**What the reviewers found.** The spec's central promise is accountability: afterwards, someone can tell who had a scope when its mirror was scratched. Two gaps undercut that promise and the second objective, "a loan and its return date reach the right member". First, a telescope "shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" on the return date (AC4) whether or not anyone has checked it, so a scope left in the cupboard goes back into circulation with no condition note. Second, every member-facing step runs on email, but nothing tells the loan officer when an email can't be delivered. Around those two, the waiting list is underbuilt: no criterion lets a member join it, and an offer is never recorded or closed. The objective that a member can "see where the loan stands" has no criterion behind it after the first email. A few rules are open in ways that let two builds behave differently: which clock the 48 hours run on, whether one member can hold two scopes on the same dates, and who can cancel a loan or read the condition notes.

**Whether it holds.** The failure-cases reviewer's unchecked return is the strongest finding. It ties straight to the scratched mirror in the Problem, and the fix is one edit. I raised the failure-cases reviewer's bounced-reminder point from "worth fixing" to "needs your decision". A production spec rates "failure nobody sees" at that level, and a mistyped address fails the second objective silently, for the confirmation as well as the reminder. I downgraded two others to "worth fixing, your call":
- **Lending by the night (right-problem reviewer).** It assumes members usually want one clear night. That guesses at member behaviour, and the reviewer said so itself. The Problem describes weekend clashes.
- **Missing finder cap (ease-of-use reviewer).** It missed that the reminder already lists what goes back in the case (AC5). What's left is real but narrower: on a loan shorter than two days, that reminder would fall before the loan starts.

Separately, two reviewers converged on the unseen loan status, and two others on the waiting list. Those convergences are the strongest of the "worth fixing" items. The fact check refuted "six telescopes": the club's register reportedly lists seven. It didn't cite where, so the count is worth confirming before you change it.
