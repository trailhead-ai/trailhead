The council's four reviews of the kiln booking sheet plan are back. The plan is at production maturity, read from the spec's own maturity section. No concern was waived.

**What the reviews found.** Two problems run through the reports. First, Task 1 gives each booking a place-in-line number and renumbers every later booking when one is cancelled. Second, the plan doesn't fully cover what the spec says happens around a firing: cancellation, standby offers and reminders. On the first, the buildability and failure-cases reviews reached the same race on their own: a cancellation that lands while the 18:00 reminder job is reading can tell two members the same firing is theirs. On the second, the reviews found several gaps. The reminder leaves out the 8 am load time. Opening the cancellation link cancels the booking, and the member gets no confirmation. A member offered a freed day stays on the standby list. If the reminder job doesn't run, nobody is told.

**Whether it holds.** The renumbering finding holds. It follows directly from steps Task 1 writes out. It also misses the spec, which says a kiln holds one firing per day and a second booking for the same day is refused and the next free day offered (AC2). A number that simply counts up refuses nothing. The failure-cases review rated the silent reminder job only "worth fixing". But a failure nobody can see is one of the concerns this spec's production maturity makes a "needs your decision" matter, so I have moved it up. The ease-of-use review's worry that members "may find the booking page confusing" names no concrete failure, so I moved it down. Its note that the calendar calls a freed day a "release" refers to a word the plan never uses, so it is a guess about wording that doesn't exist yet. Two items point at missed acceptance criteria more than the reviews said:
- The reminder's 8 am load time is part of the reminder the spec requires (AC5).
- The plan's cancellation task never states the 24-hour cutoff on cancelling (AC3). The failure-cases review saw this only as a missing test.

**What to do about it.** Revise the plan before any build:
- Rewrite Task 1 so each booking is identified by its kiln and day, with no stored number. Same-day bookings are then refused and the next free day offered.
- Give Task 2 the 8 am load time and a nightly email to the technician confirming that reminders went out.
- Rewrite Task 3 so the link opens a confirmation page and only cancels when the member confirms, inside the 24-hour cutoff. The member gets a confirmation afterwards, and the member offered the freed day leaves the standby list.
- Add a step deleting the membership spreadsheet from the laptop once the load is checked.
