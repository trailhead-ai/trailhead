**C1, C2 and C3 in detail**

All three are rated "would build the wrong thing, needs your decision". The spec is at production maturity (basis: stamp), and no concerns were waived.

---

**C1: anyone holding a forwarded confirmation email can cancel the booking.** I recommend changing the cancellation criterion so a cancel only takes effect once the member who booked confirms it. Without that, a member's firing can be taken away by someone they never meant to give that power to.

Worst case: the spec says "a member can cancel a booking up to 24 hours before its day" (AC3), but it never says how a cancel is tied to the person who made the booking. Members forward confirmation emails to partners, classmates or the studio group chat. Whoever holds the email cancels the booking. The day is freed and offered to the first member on standby, and the member who booked turns up to find their firing gone. The calendar's record of every change (AC6) shows a cancellation, but nothing shows who really made it.

Fix: replace the cancellation criterion (AC3) with this text:

> **AC3.** A member can cancel a booking up to 24 hours before its day. The cancellation takes effect only after it is confirmed from a link sent to the email address the booking was made with; the day is then freed and offered to the first member on that kiln's standby list.

Proposed disposition: `resolved`, applying the edit above as written.

Raised by: security

---

**C2: the spec books a whole kiln for a whole day, but a firing is a shared load.** I recommend deciding before planning whether a booking reserves the kiln or reserves shelf space in a firing. The current wording builds a system that makes the studio's waiting problem worse.

Worst case: the spec says "a kiln holds one firing per day" and refuses a second booking for that kiln and day (AC2). In practice a kiln is full when its shelves are full, not when one person has claimed the day. A member with a single bowl books the large kiln for Saturday. Every other member who wanted that firing is refused and pushed to the next free day. With two kilns, a fully booked fortnight could still fire mostly empty kilns. The fact check also found 112 current members against the spec's "about 90", so demand on each day is higher than the spec assumes.

Fix: members would book shelf space in a kiln's firing, and a firing would only turn people away once its shelves are full. This rewrites the Objectives and three criteria: booking a firing (AC1), one firing per kiln per day (AC2) and cancelling a booking (AC3). Caveat: the right unit depends on something the spec doesn't record, which is how many shelves each kiln has and how often firings are shared today. The technician can answer that, and the passes could not.

Proposed disposition: `revise` (`record-only`)
  Prescription: AC1 and AC2 make the whole kiln for a day the booking unit. Change it to shelf space within a firing: AC1 books a number of shelves in a kiln's firing on a day, AC2 refuses only when that firing's shelves are full and offers the next firing with room, and AC3 frees the cancelled shelves rather than the whole day.

Raised by: right problem

---

**C3: the confirmation email doesn't tell members that work must be on the shelves by 8 am.** I recommend adding the load time to the confirmation email so members learn it when they book, not only the night before.

Worst case: the booking criterion has the confirmation email state only the booking (AC1). The load time first appears in the reminder sent the evening before the firing (AC5). A member who books in the evening for the next day, or who misses the reminder, arrives at 9 am to a kiln already packed and loses the firing. One point narrows this finding: the evening reminder (AC5) already carries the 8 am time. So the gap is members who never see that reminder, not members who are never told at all. It stays at this severity because a missed load time costs the member the whole firing.

Fix: `"and that the work must be on the kiln's shelves by 8 am on the day"` added to the end of the booking criterion (AC1), after "receives a confirmation email stating the booking".

Proposed disposition: `resolved`, applying the edit above as written.

Raised by: ease of use
