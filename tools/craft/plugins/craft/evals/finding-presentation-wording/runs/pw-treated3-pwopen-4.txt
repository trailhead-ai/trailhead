The spec review (gauntlet) of the telescope loan desk spec: all eight passes are in. Maturity is `maturity: production (basis: stamp)`, and no concerns were waived.

**What the passes found.** The spec's central promise is that every change to a telescope's record can be checked afterwards, so nobody is left asking who had the scope when the mirror was scratched. That promise has a hole at the return. A telescope comes back as free on the loan calendar whether or not anyone wrote down its condition. A second gap is email. The member's email address is the only channel to them, and nothing tells anyone when a message to it doesn't arrive. Beyond those two, the passes found loose ends around the waiting list and the 48-hour cancel window. They also found that the Objectives promise a member can "see where the loan stands", but no criterion delivers it.

**Whether it holds.** The failure-cases pass was right on both counts and gave the sharpest finding. A telescope dropped in the cupboard with no officer present shows as free with no note, which recreates the scratched-mirror problem. That pass rated the email gap only as worth fixing. I've raised it to a decision because this spec is stamped production, and at production a failure no human would ever see needs your decision. Two other passes rated findings as decisions, and I've moved both down to "would cause trouble later, worth fixing, your call":

- **Lending by the night (right-problem pass).** The idea rests on how often members want a scope for a single night, and the pass admits nothing records that.
- **Case list in the confirmation email (ease-of-use pass).** The member already gets the list of what goes back in the case two days before the return date (AC5). The member it worried about, "not told", is in fact told.

I dropped two claims outright:

- The right-problem pass said the spec is a bigger build than the complaint needs. That misreads the Problem, which names three complaints, and the Objectives answer each one.
- The buildability pass asked whether the loan history is stored or worked out from events. That's a planning choice, not a spec defect.

The security and left-undecided passes disagree on who may read condition notes. That's a real choice and it's yours. The fact check found one real error: the equipment register lists seven telescopes, not six.

**What to do.** Make both edits below and advance the spec. Both are drafted and land as written.
