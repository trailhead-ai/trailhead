### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

- **Members are not told what goes back in the case (C1).** Fixed in the spec. The club should tell
  members what goes back in the case at the start of the loan, not only near the end, because members
  who are not told get billed for missing parts. Worst case: the spec said "A member borrows a
  telescope by giving a name, an email address, the telescope and a start date, and receives a
  confirmation email that states the loan and its return date" (AC1). A member who misses the later
  reminder hands the telescope back without its finder cap and is asked to replace it.
  Fix: "and what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap" added to
  the confirmation email in that borrowing criterion (AC1). The list matches the reminder that goes
  out "Two days before the return date, the member receives a reminder listing what goes back in the
  case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap" (AC5).
  Raised by: ease of use.
- **A loan ties up a whole telescope for the full dates asked (C2).** Accepted as a risk by the
  operator: "the club only lends whole telescopes this season". The decision was whether a member
  should borrow by the night rather than for a whole date range. The proposal was to lend by the
  night. Worst case: a loan is tied to a telescope and a start date, and "A telescope is on loan to
  one member at a time. A second request for the same telescope and dates is refused and the next
  free start date is offered instead" (AC2). So a member who wants the largest scope for one clear
  night holds it for a weekend, and others wait weeks for nights it sat unused.
  Fix (withdrawn by the override): members would book and extend by the night; this would have
  rewritten that borrowing criterion (AC1) and that one-member-at-a-time rule (AC2).
  Raised by: right problem.
- **A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note (C3).** Disputed by the
  operator: "the loan officer inspects every telescope at the door". The decision was whether a
  telescope should show as free only once someone has checked it. The proposal was yes. Worst case:
  "On the return date the loan officer records the telescope as back, with a note of its condition,
  and the telescope shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" (AC4). A telescope a
  member leaves in the cupboard would show as free with no note, the next member would find a
  scratched mirror, and nobody could say who had it.
  Fix (withdrawn by the override): a telescope would show as returned and unchecked until a condition
  note is recorded, and as free only after that; this would have rewritten that return criterion (AC4).
  Raised by: failure cases.

  Objectives promise that a member can "see where the loan stands", but nothing after that
  confirmation email (AC1) delivers it. Worst case: a member has no way to check a loan's state.
  Fix: add a criterion that gives members a page or a reply-to-email showing the loan, or drop the

  another from noon, and both satisfy the criterion. Fix: `"counted from noon on the start date"` in

- **Nobody records that a waiting-list offer was taken.** Not dispositioned. That cancellation

- **Waiting-list offers appear in the history but are never recorded.** Not dispositioned. "The loan
  history lists every loan, cancellation, return and waiting-list offer for a telescope, newest
