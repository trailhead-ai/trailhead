**The spec review (gauntlet): telescope loan desk** · maturity: production (basis: stamp) · no waivers

**What the reviewers found.** The spec handles bookings well. Its weak point is what happens after a booking, and the problem it was written to solve is one of the places that goes wrong. It promises that "nobody can tell afterwards who had a scope when its mirror was scratched" won't happen again, yet "the telescope shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" (AC4) can free a telescope on its return date even if nobody has checked it. Its emails have a similar gap. If a member types their email address wrong, nobody finds out, because nothing tells the loan officer. The same follow-up gap shows up in smaller places. The Objectives promise a member can "see where the loan stands", but no criterion delivers that. The waiting list in "offered to the first member on that telescope's waiting list" (AC3) has no criterion for joining it, recording an offer, or closing one. And the 48-hour cancellation cutoff doesn't say when the 48 hours start counting.

**Whether it holds.** The failure-cases reviewer is right about the unchecked return. That is the spec's own stated problem coming back, so it stays Critical. Its bounced-email point was filed as only worth fixing, but this spec is at production maturity, where a failure that only a member would notice needs your decision, so I raised it. Two other Criticals didn't hold up. The right-problem reviewer's "lend by the night" idea guesses how often members want a single night, and its own confidence line admits the spec doesn't say, so I moved it down to worth fixing. The ease-of-use reviewer said members are never told what goes back in the case. That misses "Two days before the return date, the member receives a reminder listing what goes back in the case" (AC5). The only real gap left is a loan shorter than two days, so I moved that down too. Where reviewers that couldn't see each other's work agreed, the findings line up: the missing "see where the loan stands" criterion, the unfinished waiting list, and the unstated cancellation clock. The fact check says the club has seven telescopes, not six, but cites no source. No criterion depends on the number. A few notes were about how to build it, such as whether the history is stored and which mail provider to use, rather than flaws in the spec, so I dropped them.

**What to do.** Make two edits and the spec advances: free a telescope only once its condition note is recorded, and show the loan officer any email that didn't arrive. The other findings below are worth a look before planning, but none of them blocks.

**Recommended outcome:** advances to `ready` this round once both edits land. Nothing needs a rewrite, and this spec replaces no earlier one.
