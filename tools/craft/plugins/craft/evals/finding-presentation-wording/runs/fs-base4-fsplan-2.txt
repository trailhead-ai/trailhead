**Council review: kiln booking sheet, first build**

`maturity: production (basis: stamp)`. The spec says pottery-booking is in production use, so the five maturity-sensitive concerns are rated at their strictest. No Non-Goal waives any of them.

**What the passes found.** The reviews raised three themes. First, how Task 1 orders bookings. Each booking gets a stored `turn` number, and every later number is shifted down when someone cancels. That makes it possible for two members to hold the same firing, which breaks "a kiln holds one firing per day; a second booking for the same kiln and day is refused and the next free day is offered instead" (AC2). Second, the cancellation flow in Task 3. Opening the emailed link cancels the booking with no confirmation step. Nothing tells the member it worked. The member who is offered the freed day stays on the standby list. Third, the 18:00 reminder job. Nobody finds out if it fails, and the reminder doesn't give the 8 am deadline that the spec requires.

**Whether it holds, and where it came from.** The buildability and failure-cases reviewers independently found the same `turn` race, and both are right. The plan writes out the renumbering step, and a booking stored by kiln and day doesn't need a turn number at all. The failure-cases reviewer rated the silent reminder job as worth fixing. I've raised it to a decision. The spec is at production maturity, where an unnoticed failure in production is a decision for you, and here the first sign of failure would be a member missing a firing. The ease-of-use reviewer's "members may find the page confusing" names no moment where a member gets stuck. I've moved it down a level. That reviewer's other point is the real gap: the reminder must list what has to be on the shelves by 8 am (AC5), and Task 2 leaves out the 8 am. The security points are concrete and fair, and they fit with the ease-of-use findings. The confirm page that stops a mail scanner from cancelling bookings is also the natural place to tell the member that the cancellation went through. Separately, Task 3 never states the cutoff from "a member can cancel a booking up to 24 hours before its day" (AC3). The missing boundary test shows that gap.

**What to do about it.** Rewrite Task 1 so a booking is identified by its kiln and day, with no turn number. That one change removes the race and enforces the one-firing-per-day rule (AC2). Rework Task 3 into a confirm page that cancels only before the 24-hour cutoff, confirms the cancellation, and removes the offered member from standby. Add a completion and missed-run alert to Task 2, and put the 8 am deadline in its email. The other items are small edits you can make while you're in those tasks.
