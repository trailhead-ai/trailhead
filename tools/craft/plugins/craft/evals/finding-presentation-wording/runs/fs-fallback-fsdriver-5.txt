## Council review of the kiln booking sheet plan

The spec is stamped at production maturity (`maturity: production (basis: stamp)`), and no concern was waived.

**What the reviewers found.** Two problems touch the core of the plan. First, Task 1 gives each booking a stored `turn` number and renumbers every later booking when one is cancelled. If a cancellation lands while the 18:00 reminder job is reading, two members can each be told the same firing is theirs. Second, nothing tells anyone when the 18:00 reminder job fails to run. The rest is about cancelling and standby. The emailed cancel link works for whoever opens it. A cancellation sends no confirmation. A member who was offered a freed day stays on the standby list. There are also gaps in what members are told: the reminder email omits the 8 am shelf deadline, and the lock reason is shown to every member. Last, the membership spreadsheet stays on the technician's laptop after launch.

**Whether it holds.** The buildability and failure-cases reviewers found the turn-number problem independently, and it holds. It also departs from the spec's own model: "a kiln holds one firing per day. A second booking for the same kiln and day is refused and the next free day is offered instead" (AC2). Task 1 queues bookings by turn instead of refusing a taken day. The failure-cases reviewer rated the silent reminder job as worth fixing. Because the spec is stamped production maturity, an invisible failure in production needs your decision, so I have moved it up. The ease-of-use reviewer's top finding, that the booking page "may be confusing at first," names no concrete failure, so I have moved it down to worth fixing. The rest of their findings are sound, and the missing 8 am deadline is a straight gap against "the member receives a reminder listing what must be on the shelves by 8 am" (AC5). The security reviewer's points are fair as stated. All of their findings are listed below. None of the reviewers noticed that Task 3 never enforces "a member can cancel a booking up to 24 hours before its day" (AC3). That rule needs a place in the plan alongside the cancellation fixes.

**What to do about it.** Rebuild Task 1 around the booked day: one row per kiln per day, refused when taken, with the next free day offered. That removes the turn number and the race together. Have the reminder job report that it ran. Then rework Task 3 as one piece: the cancel link opens a confirm page, the 24-hour limit is enforced, the member gets a confirmation, and an offer closes the receiving member's standby row. Fix the smaller email and privacy points alongside.

### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

**1. Should bookings be ordered by their day instead of a renumbered turn? I recommend yes. As written, two members can be sent to the same firing.**
Task 1 subtracts one from every later `turn` when a booking is cancelled. If that happens while the 18:00 job is reading, two rows share a turn and both members are told the firing is theirs. One of them loads a kiln they do not hold.
Fix: each kiln would hold one booking per day, a taken day would be refused with the next free day offered, and no turn number would be stored. This rewrites Task 1, changes what Task 2 reads, and brings the plan in line with AC2.
