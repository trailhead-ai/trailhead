**The spec review (gauntlet): telescope loan desk, round 1**
maturity: production (basis: stamp). Nothing was waived. All eight reviewers returned.

**What the reviewers found.** The spec covers borrowing and returns, but three of its promises have nothing behind them. The waiting list that cancelled dates are offered to has no way in: when a second request is refused and the next free start date is offered instead (AC2), nobody is put on a waiting list, and nothing says how an offer is answered or recorded. The Objectives say a member can "see where the loan stands", but no criterion after the first email delivers that. And the third complaint in the Problem, that nobody can tell who had a scope when its mirror was scratched, is still open. When the loan officer records the telescope as back with a condition note and it shows as free from that day (AC4), the criterion assumes the officer is there on the return date, so a scope left in the cupboard comes back as free with no note.

**Whether it holds.** The two strongest findings each came from two reviewers who could not see each other. Buildability and self-consistency both reached the waiting-list gap. Self-consistency and ease of use both reached the missing loan status. Each reviewer rated these "worth fixing, your call". I've raised both to "needs your decision" because two independent reviewers converging is the strongest signal I have, and because shipping a stated objective with nothing delivering it is a decision, not a rough edge. The failure-cases reviewer was right about the unchecked return. Its bounced-reminder point also goes up to "needs your decision": at production maturity, a failure nobody sees is rated that way. Two of the reviewers' own "needs your decision" findings came down. The right-problem reviewer's case for lending by the night depends on how often members want a single night, which the reviewer admits is unknown, so it moves to "worth fixing, your call". The ease-of-use reviewer said members are never told what goes back in the case, but the reminder two days before the return date already lists the telescope, eyepieces and finder cap (AC5). That leaves only short-notice loans, which are worth fixing but not a decision. The fact check found that the club's equipment register lists seven telescopes, not six. Nothing in the spec depends on the number.

**What to do.** Four edits, all made in the spec itself. Give the waiting list a way in, a way out and a record. Add a criterion for a member seeing their own loans. Show a returned scope as free only once its condition is noted. Tell the loan officer when an email bounces. I've drafted all four in full. The 24-hour limit for answering an offer is a value I picked, so check it.

**Recommended outcome:** accept the four edits below, and the spec advances to `ready` this round.
