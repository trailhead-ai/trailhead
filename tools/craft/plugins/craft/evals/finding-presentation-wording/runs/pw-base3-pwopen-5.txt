**The spec review (gauntlet)**: telescope loan desk, maturity: production (basis: stamp), no stand-downs, 8 of 8 passes returned

The passes keep finding the same weak spot: the moment a telescope comes back. The spec exists so that someone can tell who had a scope when its mirror was scratched. But the loan officer recording a telescope as back with a condition note (AC4) also makes the scope show as free from the return date, whether or not anyone has checked it. A scope left in the cupboard therefore goes back into circulation unchecked. The emails have a similar gap. The objectives promise that a loan and its return date reach the right member, but when an email doesn't arrive, nobody finds out. Around these two problems sit some smaller undecided edges:
- what happens to a waiting-list offer after a cancellation up to 48 hours before the start (AC3)
- no criterion lets a member "see where the loan stands", which the objectives promise
- who may read the loan history
- whether one member may hold two scopes at once

The fact check also found that the equipment register lists seven telescopes, not six.

The failure-cases reviewer was right about the unchecked return, and AC4's wording confirms it: "free … from that day" depends on the date, not on the note. That reviewer rated undelivered reminders as only worth fixing. At this spec's production maturity, though, a failure nobody can see needs your decision, and delivery is a stated objective, so I've raised it. The ease-of-use reviewer went too far in saying members are never told to return the finder cap. The reminder listing what goes back in the case (AC5) says exactly that, so I've logged it as a rough edge. The right-problem reviewer's case for lending by the night depends on a guess about how members use the scopes, and the Problem itself complains about weekend requests. I've lowered it to worth fixing. The self-consistency and ease-of-use reviewers both found the missing "see where the loan stands" criterion without seeing each other's work, so it is the strongest of the worth-fixing items.

My recommendation: make both edits below and advance the spec. The worth-fixing items don't block it, but two are cheap to settle before planning: the missing loan-status criterion and the telescope count.
