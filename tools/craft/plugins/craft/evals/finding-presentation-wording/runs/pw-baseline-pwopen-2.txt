## Gauntlet: telescope loan desk spec, first round

maturity: production (basis: stamp). No stand-downs.

**What the reviews found.** The spec is mostly sound, but it breaks down at the two moments it was written to fix. First, a telescope coming back. The spec promises that every change to a telescope's record can be checked afterwards, and the Problem it was written to fix is that nobody can tell who had a scope when its mirror was scratched. Yet the criterion "the loan officer records the telescope as back, with a note of its condition, and the telescope shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" (AC4) assumes the officer is there on the return date. It never says what the calendar shows when they aren't. Second, an email that never arrives. Every message to a member goes by email, and nothing tells anyone when one bounces. Behind those two sit a set of looser ends. The waiting list can be offered from but never joined or closed. The promise that a member can "see where the loan stands" has no criterion behind it. The spec never says who may cancel a loan or who may read the condition notes.

**Whether it holds.** The failure-cases review is right about the unchecked return. That gap is the spec's own Problem coming back through a side door. It rated the bounced email as "worth fixing, your call", but a failure nobody sees falls under production failure visibility. At this spec's production maturity, that concern needs your decision, so I have raised it to one. Two reviews overreached. The right-problem review wants telescopes lent by the night. That rests on a guess about how members borrow, which its own confidence line admits the spec doesn't settle, and the Problem's "same weekend" points the other way. I moved it down to "worth fixing, your call". The ease-of-use review wants the finder cap listed in the first email. But the criterion "two days before the return date, the member receives a reminder listing what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap" (AC5) already gives members that list before they hand anything back. The real risk is the reminder not arriving, and the bounced-email fix covers that. Two findings were reached independently by two reviews that couldn't see each other, which makes them the strongest of the Importants: the undelivered "see where the loan stands" promise (ease of use, self-consistency) and the unfinished waiting list (buildability, self-consistency). The fact check found one error. The club has seven telescopes, not six, but it rests on a register it didn't cite by line, and nothing in the spec depends on the number.

**What to do.** Make two edits to the spec. Show a returned telescope as free only once its condition is noted, and add a criterion that undelivered emails reach the loan officer. With those in, the spec can move to ready. The Importants are worth a pass before planning, especially the waiting list and who may cancel. None of the reviews asked about loans already written in the paper ledger on the day the online ledger goes live, so you may want to say a word about that too.

**Recommended outcome:** the spec moves to ready this round, once you accept the two edits.

| id | finding | proposed disposition | proposed edit |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | A telescope left back in the cupboard with no officer present shows as free with no condition note, so damage is found by the next member and can't be traced (failure cases) | `resolved` | Rewrite AC4 so a telescope shows as free only after its condition note is recorded, and as "returned, unchecked" with the last borrower named until then |
| C2 | A confirmation, reminder or offer email sent to a mistyped address is never seen, and nobody knows (failure cases; raised for production maturity) | `resolved` | Add AC7 so an undelivered email is shown to the loan officer |

The text each edit would put in the spec, already written:
- **AC4:** "When a telescope comes back, the loan officer records it as back with a note of its condition, and it shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that moment. A telescope past its return date with no condition note shows as returned and unchecked, naming the member who last had it."
- **AC7:** "When a confirmation, reminder or waiting-list offer email cannot be delivered, the loan officer sees which member and which loan it was for."

**Worth fixing, your call (10):** "see where the loan stands" has no criterion behind it (ease of use, self-consistency). The waiting list has no way in, no way to close an offer, and offers aren't recorded (buildability, self-consistency). Anyone holding a forwarded email can cancel a loan, and condition notes are shown to every member (security, left undecided). Two choices are left open: whether a member may hold two loans on the same dates, and what clock the 48 hours run on (left undecided, failure cases). Plus lending by the night, the finder cap in the first email, narrowing the Objectives to double-booking, and whether the history is stored or worked out from events.

**Rough edges, nothing to decide (3):** the telescope count (seven, not six), the confirmation email showing which dates a telescope is already taken, and "loan calendar" against "loan history" naming.

Reply "go" to accept both edits and move the spec to ready. To override a row, give the reason with it, for example "dispute C2 because …, otherwise go". You can also ask to see the full detail on any finding.
