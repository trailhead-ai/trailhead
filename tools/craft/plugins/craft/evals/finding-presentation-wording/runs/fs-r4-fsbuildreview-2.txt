**Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision**

1. **`fs-kiln/unload.py:3`** — The cool-down time comes from the kiln, not from how the firing ran. I recommend storing it on each firing, because otherwise a hot firing goes on the board before it is safe to open.
   Worst case: the change says a firing that ran hotter needs longer to cool. The code gives every firing in the large kiln 18 hours and every firing in the small kiln 10 hours. A high-temperature firing in the large kiln shows as ready at 18 hours, and someone opens a kiln that is still too hot.
   Fix: each firing would show as ready only after its own cool-down time, so two firings in the same kiln can become ready at different times. My estimate, from the cited code, is about 2 files and 2 functions, chiefly `cool_hours` and `unload_time`, plus the test's firing records. The table of cool-down times per kiln goes away.
   Raised by: code review

**Would cause trouble later, worth fixing, your call**
