### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

**1. Task 1's renumbering of bookings on every cancellation.** I recommend replacing the turn number with the booked day itself. The risk is two members turning up for one firing, which is the problem this spec exists to solve. Suppose a cancellation lands while the 18:00 reminder job is reading bookings. Two rows briefly share a `turn`, and both members are told the same firing is theirs. One of them loads a kiln they do not hold.
Fix: each member would hold one day on one kiln, and a cancellation would never move anyone else's booking. This rewrites Task 1 and touches how Tasks 2 and 5 read bookings.
*Raised by buildability and failure cases.*

**2. The 18:00 reminder job can fail without anyone noticing.** I recommend adding a signal that reaches the technician. A firing could be missed before anyone knows reminders have stopped. The job fails or never starts, no reminders go out, and nothing says so. A member misses the 8 am load and loses the firing.
Fix: the technician would get an email each evening once reminders have gone out, and a second one if none has arrived by 19:00. This changes Task 2.
*Raised by failure cases. It was rated "worth fixing" and is raised here because this spec is at production maturity.*

### Would cause trouble later, worth fixing, your call

**3. Opening the cancel link cancels the booking.** I recommend making the link open a confirmation page. Otherwise members lose firings they never meant to cancel. A member forwards the email, or their mail scanner follows the link, and the booking is cancelled without them knowing.
Fix: members would land on a page asking them to confirm before anything is cancelled. This changes Task 3.
*Raised by security.*

**4. A member who cancels gets no confirmation.** I recommend confirming on screen and by email. Otherwise a member can't tell whether their booking still stands. A member clicks by mistake, sees nothing, and either turns up for a day they no longer hold or stays away from one they still do.
Fix: members would see and receive a one-line confirmation that the booking is cancelled. This changes Task 3.
*Raised by ease of use.*

**5. The reminder leaves out the 8 am load time.** I recommend adding it. The reminder is supposed to list what must be on the shelves by 8 am (AC5). Without the time, a member arrives at 9, finds the kiln already packed, and loses the firing.
Fix: `"Your work must be on the shelves by 8 am."`, added to the reminder email in Task 2.
*Raised by ease of use.*

**6. The member offered a freed day stays on the standby list.** I recommend closing their standby place when the offer goes out. Otherwise the same member keeps getting offered days. The next cancellation offers a second day to the member who just received one, and the next person in line is skipped.
Fix: once a freed day is offered to a member, that member would no longer appear on the standby list. This changes Task 3.
*Raised by buildability.*

**7. The membership spreadsheet stays on the technician's laptop after the import.** I recommend deleting it once the load is checked. It holds 90 names and email addresses. The laptop is lost or shared, and the members' contact details go with it.
Fix: the plan's note on loading members would say the spreadsheet is deleted once the load is verified.
*Raised by security.*

**8. Members may find the booking page confusing at first.** The reviewer recommends a help page, and I recommend treating this as optional. No concrete failure was named. I lowered it from "needs your decision" for that reason.
Fix: "add a help page" (the reviewer's suggestion).
*Raised by ease of use.*

### Rough edges, nothing to decide
