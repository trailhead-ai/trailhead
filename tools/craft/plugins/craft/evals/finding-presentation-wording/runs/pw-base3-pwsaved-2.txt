### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

**Members are not told what goes back in the case (C1)** — fixed in the spec. Should the first
email a member gets say what has to go back in the case? Yes: otherwise the first time they hear
about the finder cap may be when they are asked to replace it.
Worst case: the confirmation email a member receives on borrowing (AC1) states only the loan and
its return date. The list of what goes back (the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap) only
arrives in the reminder two days before the return date (AC5). A member who misses that reminder
hands the telescope back without its finder cap and is asked to replace it.
Fix: `"and what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap"`, added to
the confirmation email (AC1).
Raised by: ease of use.

**A loan ties up a whole telescope for the full dates asked (C2)** — accepted as a risk (operator:
"the club only lends whole telescopes this season"). Should a loan hold the telescope for whole
dates, or night by night? The review recommended night by night, so the largest scope does not sit
unused.
Worst case: a member who wants the club's largest scope for one clear night holds it for a whole
weekend, while others wait weeks for a night when the scope sat unused. A member borrows by giving a
start date (AC1), and one member at a time holds the telescope for those dates (AC2).
Fix (withdrawn by the override): members would book a telescope by the night and could add nights.
This would rewrite the borrowing criteria (AC1, AC2).
Raised by: right problem.

**A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note (C3)** — disputed (operator:
"the loan officer inspects every telescope at the door"). Should a returned telescope show as free
before anyone has checked it? The review recommended no: otherwise a scratched mirror cannot be
traced to the member who had it.
Worst case: the loan officer records a telescope as back, with a condition note, on the return date
(AC4). A member who leaves the telescope in the cupboard when the officer is not there gets no note.
The calendar shows the telescope as free, and the next member finds the scratched mirror with nobody
able to say who had it.
Fix (withdrawn by the override): a telescope would show as free only once a condition note is
recorded, and as "returned, unchecked" until then. This would rewrite the return-date recording
criterion (AC4).
Raised by: failure cases.
