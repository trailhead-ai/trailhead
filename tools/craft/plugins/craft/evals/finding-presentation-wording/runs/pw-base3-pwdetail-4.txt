**Members are not told what goes back in the case (C1)**
*Proposed outcome: fixed in the spec.*

When a member borrows a scope, the email they get should tell them what has to go back in the case. I recommend adding that list to the confirmation email, because otherwise a member can lose the finder cap without knowing they were meant to keep it. The borrowing confirmation (AC1) sends only the loan and its return date. The list of what goes back (the telescope, its eyepieces and the finder cap) first reaches the member in the reminder sent two days before the return date (AC5).

Worst case: a member opens the case at home, puts the finder cap aside because nobody told them it counts, and loses it in the first week. The reminder arrives too late to help. If they typed their email address wrong, the reminder never arrives at all. The scope comes back without its cap and the member is asked to replace it. The failure-cases reviewer raised the bounced reminder separately, which shows the reminder can't be the only place this list appears.

Fix: `"and what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap"` added to the end of the borrowing criterion, so the confirmation states the loan, its return date and the case contents (AC1). The reminder before the return date (AC5) stays as it is.

*Raised by: ease of use; the failure-cases reviewer reached the same gap from the reminder side.*

**A loan ties up a whole telescope for the full dates asked (C2)**
*Proposed outcome: needs a rewrite (`record-only`).*

You need to decide what a single loan covers. I recommend lending by the night, with members able to add nights. As written, one clear night of viewing locks the scope for a whole start-to-return span, and the double-booking the spec exists to fix comes back as long waits.

Worst case: a member wants the club's largest scope for Saturday night. The only way to borrow it is from a start date to a return date (AC1 and AC2), so it is held Friday to Monday. A second member asking for Sunday night is refused, and the next free date they are offered is weeks away, while the scope sat in someone's car boot on the night they wanted.

Fix: members would book a telescope by the night and add nights as needed, and a refusal would offer the next free night. This rewrites four criteria:
- the borrowing request (AC1), which becomes nights instead of a start date
- the one-member-at-a-time refusal (AC2), which works per night
- the 48-hour cancellation (AC3), which counts from the first night booked
- the return-day check-in (AC4), which happens the morning after the last night

The reminder before the return date (AC5) needs its timing restated, because "two days before" makes no sense for a one-night loan. The Objectives are not touched. Caveat: the reviewer rated its own confidence medium because the spec never says how long loans usually run. The Problem's "same weekend" wording suggests weekend loans may be the norm, and in that case lending by the night changes less than it seems.

*Raised by: right problem.*

**A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note (C3)**
*Proposed outcome: fixed in the spec.*

A returned telescope shouldn't show as free until someone has checked its condition. I recommend adding a "returned, unchecked" state, because without one the spec doesn't fix the scratched-mirror problem it was written for. The check-in criterion (AC4) assumes the loan officer is there on the return date. A member who drops the scope in the cupboard on a day the officer is away leaves a telescope that shows as free with no condition note behind it.

Worst case: a member returns a scope with a scratched mirror while the loan officer is away. The calendar shows it as free, the next member takes it out, and the damage is found then. The loan history (AC6) has no condition note from the earlier return, so nobody can tell whose loan did it. That is exactly the gap the Problem section describes.

Fix: the check-in criterion (AC4), reworded to:

    On the return date the loan officer records the telescope as back, with a note of its
    condition. Until a condition note is recorded, the telescope shows on the club's loan
    calendar as returned and unchecked, and it shows as free only from the day the note is
    recorded.

*Raised by: failure cases.*
