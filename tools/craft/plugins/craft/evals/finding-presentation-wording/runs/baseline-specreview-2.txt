# Gauntlet: allotment waiting-list spec, round 1

maturity: production (basis: stamp). No stand-downs.

**What the passes found.** The spec describes the online queue well but ignores two things it relies on. First, it never says how the people already on the paper list get onto the online queue. A person joins by giving an email address (AC1), so anyone without one has no way back in, and anyone who re-joins loses years of waiting. Second, everything rests on the offer email arriving. If it bounces or goes to spam, the plot sits empty for 14 days, then the person who never saw the offer goes to the back (AC3) without being told. The smaller findings cluster around one gap. The Objectives promise that a person can "see their place", but only the confirmation email delivers that. The spec also never says who marks a plot as freed, or what happens when a site's queue is empty.

**Whether it holds.** The undelivered-offer finding is the strongest of the run. Reliability and Advocate reached it separately, and neither could see the other's report. The premise pass was right that existing waiters are stranded, but its claim that "a large part" of them lack email is a guess. The spec doesn't record the share, and the pass rated its own confidence medium. I've kept that finding at Critical on the part that isn't a guess: the paper list is being retired with no route across, and at production maturity migration is Critical. The fact pass found the list is larger than stated: the 3 March committee minutes record 412 names, not about 340. The premise pass also argued that AC1 to AC5 are more than the complaint needs. That overreaches, because the Problem names both complaints: nobody can see where they stand, and nobody can check the order. Divergence suggested showing the queue's history to the committee only. Don't take that as written: it contradicts the Problem, where it is members who can't check the order. Security's note that the confirmation email reveals a person's place is the feature working as intended, not a defect.

**What to do.** Add criteria that bring the paper list across in its current order and let a warden enter someone by phone number. Add criteria so that a bounced offer moves on at once without costing the person their place, and an unanswered one gets a reminder and a lapse notice. Count the 14 days from when the offer is sent. Correct the list size to 412. With those edits in, the spec can advance.

**Recommended outcome:** advance to `ready` this round. Both Criticals are `resolved` and none is `revise`.
