### Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision (1)

**`fs-kiln/unload.py:3`**: The cool-down time comes from the kiln's size, not from how the firing ran. I recommend storing the cool-down hours on each firing record and deleting the size table. Otherwise the board can tell someone a kiln is safe to open before it has cooled.

Worst case: two firings go into the large kiln, and one runs much hotter. The change says "the cool-down depends on how the firing ran, not only on which kiln it was in." The code gives both the same 18 hours (`COOL_HOURS = {"large": 18, "small": 10}`). After 18 hours the hotter one shows "ready to unload" while it is still too hot.

Fix: Add a cool-down field to each firing record and have `unload_time` read it instead of `COOL_HOURS`. Every record would need that field, so whoever creates the records has to start filling it in.
