**What the reviewers found.** The serious problem is in Task 1. The plan gives each booking a `turn` number and renumbers every later booking when one is cancelled. Two reviewers worked out the same failure on their own: if a cancellation renumbers rows while the 18:00 reminder job is reading them, two bookings can end up with the same place in line. The second theme is the reminder job itself. Nothing tells anyone if it fails to run, and when it does run its email leaves out the 8 am load time. The third theme is cancellation. Opening the link in the email cancels the booking straight away. A forwarded email or a mail scanner that follows links can cancel someone's firing, the member gets no confirmation, and the member who is offered the freed day stays on the standby list. The rest are smaller handling and wording points.
