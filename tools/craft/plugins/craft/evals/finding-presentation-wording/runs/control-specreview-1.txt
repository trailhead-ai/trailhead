**Gauntlet: the allotment waiting list spec, round 1.** Calibration: maturity: production (basis: stamp). No concern was waived.

**What the passes found.** Most of the findings come back to one assumption the spec never states: that everyone on the list can be reached by email, and that every email arrives. The way onto the list requires one: "A person joins the waiting list for one site by giving a name, an email address and the site they want" (AC1). Offers go only by email (AC2), and silence is punished: "An offer not accepted within 14 days passes to the next person in the queue, and the person who did not answer goes to the back of the queue" (AC3). The spec also never says how the people already on the paper list, in their current order, get onto the online one. The rest is smaller gaps. Nothing says who marks a plot as freed. The promise in the Objectives that a person can "see their place" has nothing behind it after the first email. And there are a few choices any build could make either way.

**Whether it holds.** The failure-cases and ease-of-use passes reached the same finding from opposite ends without seeing each other. One looked at a bounced email, the other at a member who simply never hears anything. That agreement is the strongest signal in this run. The right-problem pass is right that people without email are shut out. It guessed at how many there are, but the problem doesn't depend on that number: AC1 is the only way onto the list, so anyone without email can't get on. Underneath, it is the move off paper that the spec leaves unaddressed, and at production maturity that needs your decision. The fact check corrected the list size (412 names, not about 340). The ease-of-use pass carried the wrong number into its own finding, which didn't change its point. I cut the editorial notes: naming a mail provider, "lapse" versus "expired", and one term the self-consistency pass itself said was used consistently.

**What to do.** Add criteria that bring the paper list across in its current order and give people without email a phone route. Then add criteria so a failed or unread offer email never costs anyone their place without a warden or the person being told. Both are edits to this spec. Nothing needs another round.

**Recommended outcome:** advance to `ready` this round.
