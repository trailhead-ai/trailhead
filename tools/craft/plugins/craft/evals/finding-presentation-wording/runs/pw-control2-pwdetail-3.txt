**Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision**

**C1: Members are not told what goes back in the case.** Should a borrower be told when they book exactly what must come back with the telescope? I recommend yes, because otherwise the club ends up chasing members for lost finder caps. The spec says a member gets a confirmation email "that states the loan and its return date" (AC1), and the list of what goes back (the telescope, its eyepieces and the finder cap) only arrives in a reminder two days before the return date (AC5). If that one reminder doesn't arrive, for example because the email address was mistyped (a separate concern from the failure-cases review), the member never sees the list. They hand the telescope back without its finder cap and get asked to replace something nobody told them to keep.
Fix: `"…receives a confirmation email that states the loan, its return date, and what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap."` replaces the end of AC1, so the list arrives when the loan is booked. The reminder in AC5 stays as it is. (small)
*Raised by: ease of use*

**C2: A loan ties up a whole telescope for the full dates asked.** Should the club lend a telescope by the night, not from a start date to a return date? I recommend deciding this before anything is built, because it changes what a loan is. Every other criterion is built on top of that choice. The spec ties a loan to one telescope, a start date and a return date: a member borrows by giving "the telescope and a start date" (AC1), and a second request "for the same telescope and dates is refused" (AC2). Say a member wants the club's largest scope for one clear night. They book it for a weekend, it sits unused for the other nights, and members who asked for it later are told the next free date is weeks away.
Fix: a member would book the nights they want and could add more, and the calendar would show single nights as free or taken. This rewrites the Borrowing section's three criteria (AC1, AC2, AC3) and changes two criteria that key off the return date: the condition check on the return date (AC4) and the reminder two days before it (AC5). One caveat: the review couldn't tell how often members actually borrow for a single night. If most loans really are weekend-long, this costs a rewrite and fixes little.
*Raised by: right problem*

**C3: A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note.** Should a returned telescope stay off the calendar until someone has checked its condition? I recommend yes, because this is the exact failure the spec was written to fix. The spec has the loan officer record the telescope as back "with a note of its condition", and it "shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" (AC4). That only works if the loan officer is there on the return date. Say a member leaves a scope in the cupboard and goes home. The calendar shows it as free with no condition note. The next member finds the scratched mirror, and nobody can say who had it, which is the problem the spec opens with. With the spec at production maturity, this matters.
Fix: a telescope dropped off unchecked would show as "returned, unchecked" on the loan calendar, and would only show as free once a condition note is recorded. This rewrites AC4. The loan history's entries "with the date and the condition note" (AC6) would then also show the unchecked return. (in between)
*Raised by: failure cases*
