**The spec review: detail on the three findings that need your decision**

All three would build the wrong thing, so each needs your decision.

---

**Members are not told what goes back in the case (C1)**: fixed in the spec

Should the confirmation email also list what goes back in the case? I recommend it. Otherwise the only time a member hears that the finder cap must come back is one reminder near the end of the loan, and a member who misses that reminder pays for a cap.

Worst case: the spec sends the case list only in the reminder, "Two days before the return date, the member receives a reminder listing what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap" (AC5). The first email says only "A member borrows a telescope by giving a name, an email address, the telescope and a start date, and receives a confirmation email that states the loan and its return date" (AC1). I checked this against the spec, and the gap is narrower than the ease-of-use reviewer put it, because the reminder does carry the list. But a member who mistyped their email address never gets that reminder. Nobody told them when they collected it to check the cap was in the case. So the telescope comes back without it, and the member is asked to replace a cap that may already have been missing when they took it.

Fix: `", and lists what goes back in the case: the telescope, its eyepieces, and the finder cap"` added at the end of that confirmation-email criterion (AC1), so the member has the list on the day they collect. That two-day reminder (AC5) stays as written.

Raised by: ease of use. The failure-cases reviewer separately raised the reminder lost to a mistyped address, rated worth fixing, your call.

---

**A loan ties up a whole telescope for the full dates asked (C2)**: needs a rewrite (`record-only`)

Should a loan be counted by the night instead of as one block from a start date to a return date? I recommend rewriting the spec that way. As written, a member who wants the largest scope for one clear night holds it for the whole block, and other members are turned away from nights when it sits unused.

Worst case: a member borrows with "the telescope and a start date" (that borrowing criterion, AC1), and "A telescope is on loan to one member at a time. A second request for the same telescope and dates is refused and the next free start date is offered instead" (AC2). The spec never says who sets the return date. A member who wants Saturday night takes the club's largest scope from Friday to Sunday. A second member asks for Sunday, is refused, and is offered a date weeks away, while the scope sits in the cupboard on Sunday.

Fix: members would book a telescope by the night and could add nights, and a refused request would be offered the next free night instead of the next free start date. This rewrites the whole Borrowing section and both Returns criteria that hang off the return date: the borrowing request (AC1), the one-member-at-a-time refusal (AC2), "A member can cancel a loan up to 48 hours before it starts. The dates are freed and offered to the first member on that telescope's waiting list" (AC3), the return check-in (AC4) and the two-day reminder (AC5). The last two would count from the last booked night. Caveat: the right-problem reviewer was only moderately confident, because the spec doesn't say how often a loan is for a single night. If you know members mostly borrow for a whole weekend, reply "answer C2:" followed by what you know, and I'll re-assess it.

Raised by: right problem.

---

**A scope left in the cupboard shows as free with no condition note (C3)**: fixed in the spec

Should a returned telescope stay off the free list until someone has checked it? I recommend yes. Otherwise the spec's own goal, knowing who had a scope when its mirror was scratched, fails the first time a member drops a telescope off while the loan officer is away.

Worst case: the spec says "On the return date the loan officer records the telescope as back, with a note of its condition, and the telescope shows as free on the club's loan calendar from that day" (AC4). That ties "free" to the date, not to the check. A member leaves the scope in the cupboard on Sunday with the officer away, and the calendar shows it free from Sunday. The next member takes it on Tuesday and finds the scratched mirror. The history has no condition note for either loan, so nobody can say who did it.

Fix: `"When a telescope comes back it shows on the club's loan calendar as returned and unchecked; once the loan officer records it as back with a note of its condition, it shows as free from that day."` replaces the whole of that return criterion (AC4). This covers a telescope handed back when nobody is there to check it. Caveat: if a telescope is still unchecked when its next loan is due to start, that loan waits for the officer's check.

Raised by: failure cases.
