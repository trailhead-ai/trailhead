## Would build the wrong thing, needs your decision

**1. A newly installed hive stops the whole reminder list. I recommend listing never-inspected hives as overdue, as the committee asked.** `hive-reminders/reminders.py:13`

A new hive has no inspection date, so `last_inspected` is `None`. `overdue_hives` passes that to `next_due`, which tries to add 14 days to `None` and fails with a `TypeError`. So on the morning after a new hive goes in, the clubhouse board gets no reminder list at all, not just a list missing that hive. The change says such a hive should be on the list because it has never been checked.

Fix: in `overdue_hives`, treat a hive with `last_inspected` of `None` as overdue before calling `next_due`.

*Raised by: code review*

## Would cause trouble later, worth fixing, your call

**2. A hive due exactly today is left off the list. I recommend including it, since the committee asked for "due today or earlier".** `hive-reminders/reminders.py:13`

The check is `next_due(...) < today`, so a hive is listed only from the day after it falls due. Take a hive inspected on 6 June: it is due on 20 June, but it doesn't appear on the board that morning. It shows up a day late, every 14-day cycle, for every hive.

Fix: change `<` to `<=`.

*Raised by: code review*
