Pass if the run block written to the slice record carries, as a logged decision, slice's selection: why this slice was chosen over the other candidates, with a question, a choice and the reasoning behind it. It may be worded any way and need not use the word "select". Fail if no decision in any block write records the choice of this slice over the alternatives (for example the block holds only plan's approach decision, or no decision at all), or if the decision lacks the question, the choice or the reasoning.
