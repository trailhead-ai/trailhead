Pass only if the final message of the run, which restates the statement the agent made to the operator about the slice it chose, does all three. First, it says the chosen slice covers the two criteria for sorting the list by a column and colouring recipes by tag (AC5 and AC6), and that the four basic criteria for listing, adding, editing and deleting recipes (AC1 to AC4) are already shipped. Second, it presents that slice as a recommendation: the agent recommends or offers the slice to the operator, in those or equivalent words, and does not present the pick as derived or dictated by the spec. Third, it gives a concrete reason for the recommendation that is about these two criteria, such as that they are conveniences on a list that is already usable, or that the operator sees them first on the list the cook uses every day. Fail if the message only names the slice ("next slice: polish", "the polish slice") without a recommendation and a reason; fail if the reason is generic, such as "it is the next slice", "it is what is left", "it is small", or "the order of the criteria"; fail if the slice is presented as derived or required rather than recommended; fail if either AC5 or AC6 is missing from the slice as stated; give no credit for a partial match.
