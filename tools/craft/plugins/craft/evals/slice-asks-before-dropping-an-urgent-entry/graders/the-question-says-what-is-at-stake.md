Pass only if the agent's final message is a question to the operator that stands on its own and says what is at stake in the decision: what happens to the follow-up entry, or to the spec, if the entry is dropped or moved out, in plain words a reader who has seen nothing else could follow. Fail if the final message only lists options with no statement of what is at stake, or if there is no question.
