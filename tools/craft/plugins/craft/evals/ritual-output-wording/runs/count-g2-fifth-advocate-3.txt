## Findings
- [Critical] Production failure visibility, nightly job: if the 00:05 job fails, the desk screen shows yesterday's flags and balances with no sign they are stale, so librarians wrongly clear or refuse borrowers. Suggested: show "last updated" on the screen, with a visible warning and an alert when the run is missed.
- [Critical] Migration/backfill, first run: the plan doesn't say whether fees backdate on loans already overdue at launch; if they do, a borrower with a month-old loan is refused at the desk without warning. Suggested: charge from go-live only, or give notice first, and state the rule in the plan.
- [Important] Overflow from the Critical cap, meets the stuck-state bar: no task records a payment or waiver, so a balance can't be cleared (AC3). A borrower who pays stays blocked and needs an override at every visit, and AC7's waived total has no source. Suggested: add a desk payment/waiver action that writes to the ledger.
- [Important] Overflow from the Critical cap: after-closing returns are stamped next morning, which contradicts AC5. The borrower is charged for a day after returning the item, which can push them over £10. Suggested: stamp the actual scan date.
- [Important] AC6 has no task: the checkout refusal may be the borrower's first notice. Suggested: add the reminders task before the guard goes live.
- [Important] AC4 is only partly met: a note on the borrower doesn't record who granted the override. Suggested: store the librarian's identity and show it in the history.
- [Important] AC1 is missed: the job runs at 00:05, not within one minute, and a screen left open never refreshes. Suggested: compute overdue at read time.
- [Important] AC7 has no task: there is no monthly fees report. Suggested: add one.
- [Minor] The refusal message should show the balance and the override route.

## Confidence
Medium: based on the plan and spec only; no code, desk UI or override/payment workflow was seen, and the £10 block scenarios assume current loan data.
