You will be able to have a returned item that someone has on hold put aside for the first borrower waiting for it, and that borrower will get a message saying when they must collect it by. Try it: place a hold on an item that is out on loan, return the item, and you will see it shown as on the hold shelf for that borrower and find one message sent to them that names the pickup deadline.

**The slice:** The hold shelf slice.

**Value claim:** When an item comes back and someone is waiting for it, it is kept for them instead of going back on the open shelf, and they are told once when they need to pick it up.
