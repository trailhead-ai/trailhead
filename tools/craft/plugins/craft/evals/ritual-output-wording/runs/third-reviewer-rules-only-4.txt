## Findings
- [Critical] None of the four tasks has a test contract: nothing pins the fee cap, the £10 threshold boundary (exactly £10.00 vs £10.01), or same-day return behaviour. A fee calculator can ship that goes over the replacement price, or charges on the day of return, with no test going red. Suggested: give each task a test contract written first: the fee at the cap and one day past it, a balance at and over £10, and a return scanned before and after closing.
- [Critical] Fee charging is irreversible and there is no idempotency key, dry run or backfill gate: on its first production run the nightly job charges fees on every loan that is already overdue. Re-running after a crash, or the job firing and a return being scanned on the same day, charges the same item-day twice, and the ledger is left wrong with no rollback. Suggested: key each charge by (loan, date) with a unique constraint, add a dry-run mode that reports totals before it writes, and gate the first run as a staged, replayable backfill.

- [Important] Stamping late returns with the next morning's date directly breaks "Returning an overdue item stops the fee from growing the same day, even if the return is scanned after closing time" (AC5): the 00:05 job sees the item still out and adds one more day's fee. Suggested: record the actual scan time, and attribute the return to the business day it was scanned in.
- [Important] If the nightly job fails, nothing shows it: no heartbeat, alert or last-run marker. Flags and fees silently stop updating. Suggested: emit a last-success timestamp and alert when it is more than 26h old.
- [Important] A batch run at 00:05 with no retry cannot reliably meet "a borrower whose loan passes its due date is shown as overdue on the desk screen within one minute of the date rolling over" (AC1). Suggested: work out overdue status from the due date when the screen loads, not from a stored flag.
- [Important] Storing the override as a free-text note does not capture who granted it, which "a librarian override records who granted it and the reason given" (AC4) requires. Suggested: store overrides as structured records, with the librarian's ID taken from the session.
- [Important] No task covers the reminder messages (AC6) or the monthly fees report (AC7). Waivers also have no ledger, so the report's totals cannot reconcile.
- [Minor] The checkout guard reads the balance while the job or a return may be writing it, so it can briefly accept or refuse wrongly.
