## Findings
- [Critical] None of the four tasks has a test contract: nothing pins the fee cap, the £10 threshold or the after-close return. Today the after-close return breaks AC5, "returning an overdue item stops the fee from growing the same day, even if the return is scanned after closing time". The plan stamps those returns with the next morning's date, so the 00:05 job charges one more day and no test catches it. Suggested: give each task failing-first tests on both sides of each boundary: balance £9.99, £10.00 and £10.01; fee one day under the replacement price and one day over; a return at 23:59 after closing.
- [Critical] The nightly job writes money and flags with no idempotency, signal or first-run gating. If the 00:05 run fails, nothing is charged and nobody knows. A rerun, or the fee run at return, can charge the same day twice. On first deploy, every loan already overdue picks up its back fees at once, with no preview. Suggested: key charges by (loan, date) so reruns are safe, emit a success metric or alert, and have the first run produce a dry-run report before any ledger is written.
- [Important] The plan can't meet AC1, "shown as overdue on the desk screen within one minute of the date rolling over". The job starts at 00:05, five minutes after rollover. Suggested: work out overdue at read time from the due date, or move the job to 00:00.
- [Important] The override stores only a note, so it misses AC4, "records who granted it and the reason given, and shows in the borrower's history". Suggested: add a structured override record with the librarian's ID, and a test for it.
- [Important] No task covers AC6, "one reminder message three days before a due date and one on the day after, and no other automatic messages", or AC7, the "monthly fees report" whose category totals "reconcile with the per-borrower ledgers". AC6 needs a guard against duplicate sends on reruns.
- [Important] The checkout guard has no defined behaviour when the fee balance can't be read: it could refuse every checkout or let every borrower through. Suggested: choose one and test it.
- [Minor] The plan doesn't say what happens to the overdue flag when a loan is renewed or its due date is changed after the job has run.
