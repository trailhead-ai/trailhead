You will be able to return an item that has a hold on it and have it set aside for the first borrower in its queue, with that borrower told once when they must collect it by.

Try it: once this is built, place a hold on an item that is out on loan, check the item back in, and you will see it set aside for that borrower, who will receive a single message naming the pickup deadline.

**Chosen slice:** The hold shelf slice

**Fully covered:** this slice makes two acceptance criteria fully green:
- When a held item is returned, it is set aside for the first borrower in its queue and shown as on the hold shelf (AC5).
- The first borrower is sent one message that the item is waiting, naming the pickup deadline (AC6).

**Partially covered:** none. This slice makes no acceptance criterion only partly green.

**Left of the spec once this slice is built:** two of four left in Placing a hold, one of three in Filling a hold, and three of three in Collecting at the desk. One of the two in Placing a hold is already partly covered and this slice does not finish it: a borrower can hold at most five items at once, and a sixth hold is refused with a message saying why (AC2).

**Visual surface:** this slice touches none. It changes what the system does when an item comes back and which message goes out, and it adds or changes no screen, so no enumerated states apply.

**Value claim:** items with a queue behind them stop going back onto the open shelf. Each one waits for the next borrower in line, and that borrower gets one message saying how long they have to collect it.

**Covers:** AC5, AC6
