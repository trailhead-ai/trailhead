## Findings
- [Critical] Migration and backfill: borrowers get blocked at checkout without ever being warned. The plan doesn't say whether the first nightly run charges fees for loans that are already overdue. It also leaves out the AC6 reminders (three days before due, and the day after). **What concretely fails:** on launch day a borrower with an old overdue loan arrives at the desk and is refused at Task 3. It is the first they have heard of any fee, and the librarian has no reminder history to point to. Suggested: decide on backfill now (charge from launch date only, or backdate with a grace period), build the AC6 reminders before the checkout guard goes live, and show "fees since <date>" on the desk screen.
- [Critical] Returns scanned after closing are charged an extra day, against AC5. The plan stamps these returns with the next morning's date, and the 00:05 job charges another day before then. **What concretely fails:** a borrower drops a book in the after-hours return at 18:30 and the next day sees a balance one day higher than expected. That can push them over £10 and block their checkout, and the plan gives the librarian no way to fix the charge. Suggested: use the drop-box/closing time as the effective return date, or have the nightly job skip items returned after closing.
- [Important] There is no way to waive a fee. AC7 needs "total waived" figures, but the plan only lets librarians override the checkout block. Staff can't correct a wrong charge; they can only work around it. Suggested: add a waive action that records a reason.
- [Important] The override record doesn't meet AC4. A free-text note on the borrower doesn't record who granted it, and may not show in their history. Later desk staff can't tell an approved exception from a casual note. Suggested: store a structured override event with staff ID and reason, shown in the history.
- [Important] AC1 fails as planned. The overdue flag is written at 00:05 and the screen updates only on load, not within one minute. A screen left open shows stale state. Suggested: run the job at 00:00, or work out overdue status when the screen is rendered.
- [Important] The refusal screen is undefined. The plan doesn't say what the librarian sees when checkout is refused: the balance, the items driving it, or where to override. Suggested: show the balance, a per-item breakdown and an inline override.
- [Minor] The desk screen shows a single fee balance with no per-item breakdown or cap, so the librarian can't explain the charge to the borrower.

## Confidence
Medium. I compared the plan and spec line by line, but there are no UI designs or message copy to check.
