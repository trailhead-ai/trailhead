## Findings
- [Critical] Returns scanned after closing are charged an extra day, against AC5. **What concretely fails:** a borrower drops an overdue book in the after-hours return at 19:00. The plan stamps the return with the next morning's date, so the nightly job charges one more day. That extra day can push the balance over £10, and the checkout guard then refuses the borrower's next visit for a fee they did nothing to earn. The librarian has no tool to correct the charge (see waivers below). **Suggested:** stamp the return with the actual scan time, or the last open day, and have the fee calculator use the stamped return date as the end of charging.
- [Critical] The plan builds no borrower reminders, so AC6 is missing entirely. **What concretely fails:** the borrower gets no message 3 days before the due date and none the day after. The first they hear of the fee is when checkout is refused at the desk, in front of a queue, so the librarian has to deliver unexpected bad news. **Suggested:** add a task to the nightly job that sends the D-3 and D+1 messages, with a per-loan sent-flag so each message goes out only once.
- [Important] Overrides record the reason but not who granted them, against AC4. A librarian or manager reviewing a dispute later can't tell who allowed the checkout, and a free-text "note" field may not appear in the borrower's history view. **Suggested:** store the overrider's identity and a timestamp as a structured history entry.
- [Important] The overdue flag cannot appear within a minute of rollover, against AC1. The nightly job runs at 00:05, so the flag lands at least 5 minutes late. User impact is low because the desk is closed then, but the criterion fails as written. **Suggested:** run the job at 00:00, or work out overdue status when the screen loads.
- [Important] There is no way to waive a fee, and the plan does not build the AC7 report. Without a waiver, AC7's "total waived" can never be anything but zero, and staff can't fix a wrong charge, such as the after-hours one above, except by overriding again on every visit. **Suggested:** add a waive action that records who and why, plus the monthly report.
- [Minor] The plan doesn't say what the refused-checkout screen shows. It should give the balance, the £10 threshold and the override action in one place, not a bare "refused".
- [Minor] The balance only refreshes when the screen loads, so after a return at the desk the librarian sees a stale figure until they reload.

## Confidence
High. Each finding comes from a direct conflict between plan.md (lines 6–11) and the spec's AC1, AC4, AC5, AC6 and AC7.
