**The override slice**

You will be able to lift a fee block for a borrower stuck at the desk, with a record of who lifted it and why. Try it: once it's built, take a borrower owing more than ten pounds, confirm that checkout is refused, grant an override with a reason, then check out the item and find the override in the borrower's history.

**What it makes fully green:**
- A borrower owing more than ten pounds in late fees cannot check out further items until the balance is cleared or a librarian overrides (AC3).
- A librarian override records who granted it and the reason given, and shows in the borrower's history (AC4).

**What it makes only partly green:** nothing. This slice claims no partial coverage.
