You will be able to lift a fee block at the desk for a borrower who is stuck, and the system will keep a record of who lifted it and why. Try it: once this is built, you'll take a borrower owing more than ten pounds, see their checkout refused, grant an override with a reason, and then find your name and that reason in their history.

**Chosen slice:** The override slice, the smallest next slice above the value floor.

**Fully covered:**
- A borrower owing more than ten pounds in late fees cannot check out further items until the balance is cleared or a librarian overrides (AC3).
- A librarian override records who granted it and the reason given, and shows in the borrower's history (AC4).

**Partly covered:** None; this slice makes no criterion only partly green.

**Left after this slice:** Five of the seven criteria will remain. They concern showing loans as overdue, charging the daily fee, stopping the fee on return, reminders, and the monthly fees report. None of them is partly covered. The spec text has no slice ledger, so this count starts from all seven, even though an earlier slice touched the desk screen.

**Visual surface:** This slice touches no visual surface beyond the desk screen an earlier slice already covered, so no enumerated states apply.

**Value claim:** A fee block stops being a dead end for the borrower, because a librarian can lift it at the desk and leave an accountable record of who did so and why.

**Covers:** AC3, AC4
