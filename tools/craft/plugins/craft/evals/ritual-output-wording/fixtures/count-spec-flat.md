# Spec: Training days for the Pennine Wallers' Trust

Status: ready

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC1.** A member can book a place on a training day from the trust's events page, up to the evening before.

- **AC2.** Each training day has twelve places, and a thirteenth booking is refused with a message saying the day is full.

- **AC3.** A member who cancels at least a week ahead is refunded in full, whether they paid by card or by cheque.

- **AC4.** A booked member is sent one reminder the evening before, saying where to meet and when the day starts.

- **AC5.** The attendance table shows every training day of the year with how many members came.

- **AC6.** The wall-length table adds up the metres built, by site and by year.

- **AC7.** The refunds table shows each refund paid, with the amount and the date.

- **AC8.** Each of the tables can be downloaded as a spreadsheet for the trustees' meeting.

- **AC9.** The annual summary page shows all the tables together, with the year at the top.

- **AC10.** The booking confirmation tells the member which boots and gloves to bring on the day.

- **AC11.** An injury reported on the day is recorded, with the insurer's form filled in from the booking.

- **AC12.** A member who has attended can download a certificate showing the training days they completed.

## Non-goals

Training days run by other trusts.
