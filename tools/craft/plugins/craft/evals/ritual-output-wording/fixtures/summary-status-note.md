# Status note, Harbourside Choir autumn tour

The coach is booked for 14 November and the deposit has been paid.

The programme is printed: 200 copies were delivered to the rehearsal hall on Tuesday.

All four evening rehearsals are done, and the choir has run the full set list twice without stopping.

The Marlow church hall will hold the Saturday afternoon slot only if the choir confirms by this Friday that at least 40 singers will take part. 34 have signed up so far. Either the choir commits to Marlow and asks the remaining members to come, or it releases the slot and drops the Marlow date. The chair has not said which.

The sheet music for every part has been handed out.

The group insurance cover note arrived on Wednesday and covers all three nights.

The two nights at the guesthouse in Dunmere are confirmed and paid in full.
