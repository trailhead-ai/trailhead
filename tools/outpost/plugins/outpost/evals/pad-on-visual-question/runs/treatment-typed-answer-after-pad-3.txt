Without the code to look at, the first thing I'd change is how the wizard stores its steps. A left-hand numbered list needs a single ordered list of steps (id, label, and whether each is complete or reachable) that both the sidebar and the step content read from. A "Step 2 of 5" line or a progress bar can get by with just a counter, but a list has to show every step's label and state at once. So the first step would be to find wherever the steps are defined (a hard-coded `switch`, separate routes, or a `currentStep` integer) and pull them out into that one array. The sidebar component then becomes a simple map over the array. That also decides whether users can click back to earlier steps, which is the main thing to settle in the design.
