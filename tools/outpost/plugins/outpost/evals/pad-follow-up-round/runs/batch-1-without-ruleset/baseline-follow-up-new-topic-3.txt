**Build the text badge at the end of the row.**

- **The dot and the strip show status by colour alone.** That fails accessibility guidelines (WCAG 1.4.1). Colour-blind users and grayscale printouts can't tell Paid from Overdue, and tooltips or legends are a weak fix.
- **Invoice statuses have names people use.** Draft, Sent, Paid, Overdue and Void are words people search, filter and talk about ("show me what's overdue"). A badge shows the word itself, so nobody has to decode a colour.
- **Five or more statuses is too many for colour.** A dot or strip works for two or three states. Past that, users have to learn the colours.

The badge's weakness is that it sits at the far right, away from where the eye starts each row. Two cheap ways to cover that:

1. Make it a fixed-width status column with consistent alignment, not something trailing loose after the amount. Then people can scan straight down it, and it can be sorted and filtered.
2. If overdue invoices are what users mainly look for, add a left-edge strip **for Overdue only** as well as the badge. It helps the eye spot the urgent rows without colour carrying all the meaning.
