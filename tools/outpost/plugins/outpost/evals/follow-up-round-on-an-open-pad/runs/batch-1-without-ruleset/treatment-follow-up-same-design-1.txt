I'd build **A: one line per entry**, with a click to expand the details inline.

Admins scanning hundreds of entries need as many rows on screen as possible, and columns that line up so the eye can run down one field (actor, action, result). A single line gives both, and you get about twice the rows of the two-line version. The two-line version shows details without a click, but the muted second line breaks up the columns and halves the density. Cards are easy to read one at a time but have no columns to scan or sort, so they don't suit this many entries.

A few things to build into A:
- Show the result as a coloured pill (ok/fail) so failures stand out while scrolling.
- Expand the details in place, under the row, rather than in a side panel. That keeps the user's position in the list.
- Show the full value on hover where a cell gets truncated.
