**So, for the line items: table rows (A) or cards (B)?**

- **A, table rows** (still my pick): dense, and quantities and totals line up in columns so they're easy to scan and compare. It gets cramped on narrow screens.
- **B, cards**: roomier, works well on mobile, and has space for a note on each item. You see fewer items per screen and it's harder to compare across them.

A rough rule: if invoices often run past a handful of lines, or people mostly view them on desktop, go with A. If they're short and often opened on a phone, B is worth it.
